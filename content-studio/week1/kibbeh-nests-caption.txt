Fried Kibbeh Balls Recipe, shaped like crispy nests 👀
Send it to the one who says kibbeh only has one shape.

Kibbeh is the Levantine classic of fine bulgur and lean ground lamb or beef, stuffed with browned lamb and pine nuts and fried deep brown. Same recipe, one new tool: the shell paste goes through a potato ricer and wraps each stuffed ball in crispy strands. Full recipe in the first comment.

طريقة عمل الكبة المقلية، بس بشكل عش مقرمش 👀
