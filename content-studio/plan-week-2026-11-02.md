## Rules for this week (each one tested above)
1. Hook: a statement, never a question: the science rule or "X is the mistake", with the book's own number.
   Rotate the wording (rule / mistake / cause and effect), keep the family.
2. Caption: English first, keyword first (SEO rule), the rule as the title line, Arabic block, emotional purchase
   line without price, 3-5 hashtags.
3. Video: the tested chef format A (read from the winners' files): the cook's face in a medium shot at frame one
   already doing the hero step, 4-7 shots of 2-4 s, a slow-motion macro money shot, the cook presents the food at the
   end; one small caption line (~44 px) on the black apron. Not the fast-cut hands-only style (kibbeh 0.3x, sushi
   speed run 0.4x). Format B (motion transfer) only with a viral reference reel.
4. Engagement round 30 minutes before posting (sweet potato), list in `engage-qatar-uae.md`.
5. 4 reels, no carousel; one story teaser with a countdown sticker on each reel day.
6. Post at 22:30, the sweet potato's time.
