Baked Eggplant Chicken Buns Recipe | No Bread | خبز الباذنجان بالدجاج والجبنة
Send this to someone who says they don't like eggplant.

Thick eggplant rounds salted for 10 minutes so they never turn watery, then mozzarella, a filling of minced chicken, spinach and ricotta, more mozzarella and a second round on top. Brushed with egg, covered in a crumb and cheese crust and baked next to cherry tomatoes and whole garlic. About 42 g of protein in every bun.
