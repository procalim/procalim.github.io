===== DM 1: THIS RECIPE (N° 020 Tanghulu (Microwave Method), p167, word for word; our tray marked) =====
Here it is 🍇 Ice Cubes That Aren't (Tanghulu, N° 020 in my book)
Grapes (washed and completely dried), 1 cup white sugar, ½ cup water. Ours: a silicone ice-cube tray in place of the bamboo skewers.
1. (Ours) Set one dry grape in each cell of the tray. Critical: fruit must be 100% dry or sugar won't stick.
2. In a microwave-safe mug, mix sugar and water. Do NOT stir after mixing.
3. Microwave in 30-second intervals. Usually takes 5–8 minutes total.
4. Test: dip a chopstick in sugar then in ice water. If it crunches, it's ready.
5. (Ours) Working fast, pour the hot syrup over the grapes to fill each cell, and set the tray in ice water. Pop the cubes out when hard.
Chef's note: work extremely fast, the sugar sets within seconds of touching the fruit.
Careful: the syrup is very hot.
