===== DM 1a: THE HASHBROWN (N° 110, word for word; Instagram's DM limit is 1,000 characters, so the recipe is two messages) =====
Here it is 🥔🌯 Hashbrown Crunchwrap (N° 110 + N° 008 in my book). Part 1, the hashbrown:
Hashbrown: 2 large Russet potatoes (peeled), 1 Egg, 1 tbsp Cornstarch, 1 tsp Onion powder, Salt and Black pepper, Oil spray.
1. Grate the potatoes using a box grater.
2. Place the grated potatoes in a clean kitchen towel and squeeze as hard as you can to remove all the liquid (dry potatoes equal crispy hashbrowns).
3. In a bowl, mix the dry grated potatoes with the egg, cornstarch, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
4. Preheat a waffle iron to high heat and spray generously with oil. Place a handful of the potato mixture into the waffle iron, close the lid, and cook for 5–8 minutes until deeply golden brown and crunchy.

===== DM 1b: THE WRAP (N° 008, word for word; the hashbrown layer is ours) =====
Part 2, the wrap: 1 Large flour tortilla. Zone 1: Burger sauce or Mayo. Zone 2: Grilled Chicken (ours: the hashbrown on top). Zone 3: Lettuce and Tomato. Zone 4: Cheese slices.
1. Lay tortilla flat. Make one cut from center down to the bottom edge. Place one ingredient per quadrant.
2. Fold bottom-left quadrant up over top-left, then over to top-right, then down to bottom-right. You now have a triangle.
3. Toast in a panini press or skillet until golden and cheese melts.
