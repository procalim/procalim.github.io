===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Beef Shawarma, the Oven Tower (makes 4 wraps)
Beef: 800 g half-frozen sirloin (halal), 100 g plain yogurt, juice of 1 lemon, 1 tbsp white vinegar (not wine vinegar), 4 garlic cloves, 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp each cumin, paprika and ground coriander, ½ tsp each cinnamon, cardamom, allspice and black pepper, 1½ tsp salt, ½ onion, 1 long skewer
Toum: 6 garlic cloves, ½ tsp salt, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 100 ml oil, 2 tbsp ice water
To serve: 4 thin Arabic breads, pickles, 2 tomatoes, 1 red onion with sumac and parsley
1. Slice the beef as thin as you can.
2. Toss it with the marinade; leave it 2 hours.
3. Stand the skewer in the onion on a lined tray; stack the slices into a tower.
4. Roast at 220°C for 45-50 min.
5. Shave thin slices; sear on a smoking-hot griddle 1-2 min.
6. Toum: blend garlic, salt and lemon; stream in the oil and ice water.
7. Bread, toum, pickles, tomato, onion, beef; roll tight, press on the griddle until crisp, cut.
About 51 g protein a wrap.
