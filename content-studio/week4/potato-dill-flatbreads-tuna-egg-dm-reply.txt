===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Potato Dill Flatbreads with Tuna & Egg (makes 4)
Cups: 600 g potatoes, boiled and peeled, 80 g flour, 100 g shredded mozzarella, a small bunch of dill, a pinch of salt
Filling: 2 tins of tuna in water (320 g drained), 2 tbsp Greek yogurt, 1 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tsp harissa, 1 tsp paprika, a squeeze of lemon
Top: 4 eggs fried in 1 tbsp oil, basil leaves, lime
1. Mash the warm potatoes with the flour, mozzarella, dill and salt into a soft dough.
2. Roll 4 balls and press the bottom of a glass into each to make a cup.
3. Bake at 200°C for 25-30 min, until crisp underneath.
4. Mix the tuna with the yogurt, tomato paste, harissa, paprika and lemon.
5. Fry the eggs, keeping the yolks runny.
6. Fill each cup with tuna, top with an egg and basil, serve with lime.
About 37 g protein a cup.
