Shredded cooked chicken, squeezed dry and mixed with eggs and a little cheese, pressed into the tray and scored into squares. It bakes bare until dry and golden, and only then the sauce goes on, one topping in every square, mozzarella, and back in the oven until it bubbles. Cut along the lines.
