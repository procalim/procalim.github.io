Here's your recipe 🤍 The "Dense Bean Salad", Protein from the Book Ep 3
Ingredients: 1 can garbanzo beans (chickpeas), rinsed; 1 can cannellini beans (white beans), rinsed; 1 cup cherry tomatoes (halved); 1 bell pepper (diced); 1/2 red onion (finely diced); 1/2 cup feta cheese (crumbled); 1/4 cup halal beef salami (diced small); fresh parsley and basil (chopped)
Dressing: 1/3 cup olive oil, 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 clove garlic (minced), salt, pepper, oregano
1. In a massive bowl, combine the beans, tomatoes, pepper, onion, cheese, meats, and herbs.
2. In a small jar, vigorously shake the dressing ingredients together until emulsified.
3. Pour the dressing over the bean salad and toss aggressively.
4. Let it marinate in the fridge for at least 30 minutes before eating. The longer it sits, the better it tastes!
Halal swaps: beef salami, apple cider vinegar, halal-certified feta.
