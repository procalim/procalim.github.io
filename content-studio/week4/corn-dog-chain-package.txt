===== DM 1: THE RECIPE (English) =====
Here's the recipe 🤍 Corn Dog Chain
Hot dogs (halal beef) and mozzarella sticks, skewers
Batter: 1 cup flour, 2 tbsp sugar, ½ tsp salt, 1 egg, ¾ cup cold milk, 2 tsp baking powder
Panko breadcrumbs, sugar (for coating)
0. (our chain) Cut one long slit through the middle of each hot dog, ends uncut. Push the ends so it opens into a loop, pass the next hot dog and a mozzarella stick through it. 4 links, a skewer through the first.
1. Freeze 20 mins (helps batter stick).
2. Whisk batter ingredients until thick and sticky.
3. Coat the chain completely (a long tray works best).
4. Roll in panko breadcrumbs (press them on).
5. Deep fry at 350°F (175°C) for 3-5 minutes until golden brown.
6. Roll in sugar immediately. Top with ketchup and mustard.
