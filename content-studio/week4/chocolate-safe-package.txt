===== CAPTION =====
The Chocolate Safe 🔐 Shake it and it rattles. Pour hot milk and it opens. Save it for movie night.

Semi-sweet chocolate chips (melted)
Hot cocoa mix (powder)
Mini marshmallows (gelatin-free)
Our coins: small round golden cookies
Silicone sphere mold (ours: a safe-shaped mold in two halves)
1. Coat inside of silicone molds with melted chocolate. Freeze 5 mins. Do a second coat.
2. Gently pop chocolate half-spheres out of mold. (Ours: the two halves of the safe.)
3. Fill half the spheres with 1 tbsp cocoa powder and a handful of marshmallows. (Plus our cookie coins.)
4. Warm a plate in the microwave. Rub empty half-sphere edges on the plate to melt slightly.
5. Stick the empty half onto a filled half to seal into a ball.
6. To serve: place bomb in a mug. Pour steaming hot milk over it.
Chef's note: do two layers of chocolate coating for a bomb that doesn't crack.
Halal: gelatin-free marshmallows only.
