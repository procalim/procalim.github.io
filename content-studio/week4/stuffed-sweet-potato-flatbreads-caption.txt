Stuffed Sweet Potato Flatbreads Recipe | Beef, Spinach & Cheese | خبز البطاطا الحلوة المحشي
Send this to someone who loves melted cheese.

Baked sweet potato mashed with mozzarella and flour into a soft dough, then filled with halal ground beef, spinach and more mozzarella, twisted shut in cling film, pressed into thick rounds and pan-fried until golden. Cut one open, pull, and dip it in tomato sauce. About 31 g of protein in every flatbread.
