Lantern Corn Dog 🌭 the cheese stays locked inside till the last bite. Save it for the weekend.

Hot dogs (halal beef) and mozzarella (cut into blocks), skewers
Batter: 1 cup flour, 2 tbsp sugar, ½ tsp salt, 1 egg, ¾ cup cold milk, 2 tsp baking powder
Panko breadcrumbs, sugar (for coating)
0. (our cut) 6 slits along the middle of the hot dog, ends uncut. Push the ends together so it opens like a lantern; a mozzarella block goes inside.
1. Skewer the hot dogs and cheese. Freeze 20 mins (helps batter stick).
2. Whisk batter ingredients until thick and sticky. Pour into a tall glass.
3. Dip skewers into batter, coating completely.
4. Roll in panko breadcrumbs (press them on).
5. Deep fry at 350°F (175°C) for 3-5 minutes until golden brown.
6. Roll in sugar immediately. Top with ketchup and mustard.
Chef's note: freezing before battering is the trick; the cold helps the batter stick evenly.
