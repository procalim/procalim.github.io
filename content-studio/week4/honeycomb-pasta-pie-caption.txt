Every rigatoni tube stands upright in a round springform pan until it looks like a honeycomb. A little ricotta goes into the center of each tube, then the marinara is poured over the top and pushed down into the tubes, never mixed in. A thick layer of mozzarella, into the oven until it bubbles golden, and a 15 minute rest before slicing so every piece stands.
