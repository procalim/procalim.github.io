Chocolate Cake Recipe in a pan, no oven 🍫
Send this to the friend who says they can't bake without an oven.

Pan chocolate cake is a soft cocoa and coffee cake cooked covered in one pan on the lowest flame, then poked all over and soaked with a warm chocolate sauce until every square is wet to the middle. No oven, no cake tin, one pan. Pour the sauce while the cake is hot: it drinks it all. Comment CHOCOLATE and I'll send you the link to my book of 361 halal recipes.
