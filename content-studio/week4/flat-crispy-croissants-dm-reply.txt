Here's your recipe 🤍 Flat Crispy Croissants (Candied Croissants), N° 103 from my book
Ingredients: 2 butter croissants (day-old works best), 2 tbsp butter (melted), 3 tbsp honey or maple syrup, sugar (for dusting), powdered sugar or melted chocolate (for dipping)
1. Preheat a skillet over medium-low heat.
2. Use a rolling pin to flatten the croissants completely until they are thin like a pancake.
3. Mix the melted butter and honey together. Brush this mixture generously on both sides of the flattened croissants.
4. Place the croissants in the skillet. Place a piece of parchment paper on top, and press down hard with a heavy pot.
5. Cook for 2-3 minutes per side until they are incredibly crispy and deeply caramelized.
6. Let cool (they will harden like a candy shell) and dip half in melted chocolate.
