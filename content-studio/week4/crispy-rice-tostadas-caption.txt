Tuna marinated in soy, pressed into white and black sesame and seared for a minute a side, so the crust is crisp and the middle stays deep red. Sticky rice pressed into flat disks and fried until golden. Then cucumber, avocado, the sliced tuna, edamame and a spicy mayo drizzle. About 33 g of protein in every tostada.
