Soaked chickpeas blitzed with parsley, coriander and red onion, mixed with tahini, cumin and a little flour, pressed into cups and baked with sesame, no deep frying. Then hummus, chicken shawarma, toasted almonds, a tomato and onion salad with sumac and a tahini drizzle. About 38 g of protein in every cup.
