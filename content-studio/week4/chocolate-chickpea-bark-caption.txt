Crunchy Chocolate Chickpea Bark Recipe | Wet Chickpeas Are the Mistake | الحمص المبلول هو الغلطة
Send this to the friend who needs a crunchy snack that isn't chips.

A can of chickpeas, drained and rinsed, laid out on parchment, tossed with a little oil and sugar, then air fried until deep golden and dry. Only then the melted chocolate goes in, folded through until every chickpea is coated, spread on fresh parchment and frozen until it sets, then snapped into crunchy clusters. Three ingredients, and the crunch comes from roasting them dry first. Comment CHICKPEA and I'll send you the link to my book of 361 halal recipes.
