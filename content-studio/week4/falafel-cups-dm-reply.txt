===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Baked Falafel Cups with Hummus & Chicken Shawarma (makes 4)
Cups: 250 g dried chickpeas soaked overnight, a handful each of parsley and coriander, ½ red onion, 2 garlic cloves, 2 tbsp tahini, 40 g flour, ½ tsp baking powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, salt, 1 tbsp sesame, 1 tbsp olive oil
Filling: 200 g hummus, 300 g halal chicken breast strips with shawarma spice, lemon and garlic, 30 g toasted slivered almonds, parsley, 1 red onion and 2 tomatoes with sumac, tahini sauce
1. Blitz the chickpeas, herbs, onion and garlic.
2. Mix in the tahini, flour, baking powder, spices and salt.
3. Roll 4 balls and press the bottom of a glass into each to make a cup.
4. Sesame and oil on top, bake at 200°C for 25-30 min.
5. Cook the chicken with the spice, lemon and garlic.
6. Fill with hummus, chicken, almonds, parsley and salad, then the tahini.
About 38 g protein a cup.
