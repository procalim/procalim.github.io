Send this to someone who's low on iron.

Phyllo scrunched into ruffles and packed upright in the tray, a filling of spinach, red lentils, red pepper and nigella seeds pushed into every fold, baked until crisp. Only then the egg and yogurt pour goes in, baked until fully set, with lemon squeezed over at the end. Lentils for iron, lemon and red pepper for vitamin C, which helps the body absorb iron from plants. Have your tea an hour later, not with the meal.
