===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Crispy Mashed Potato Tacos with Beef & Guacamole (makes 4)
Flatbreads: 600 g boiled peeled potatoes, 80 g flour, 60 g shredded halal-certified mozzarella, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp salt, 1 tbsp olive oil
Beef: 400 g lean halal ground beef, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp chilli, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt, 1 tbsp oil
Guacamole: 2 avocados, juice of 1 lime, ¼ red onion, coriander, salt
Pickled onion: 1 red onion, 60 ml white vinegar (not wine vinegar), 60 ml water, 1 tsp sugar, ½ tsp salt
1. Soak the sliced onion in the pickle mix for 15 min.
2. Mash the warm potatoes with the flour, mozzarella, garlic powder and salt.
3. Roll 4 balls, oil them, press each 5 mm flat with your fingers.
4. Bake at 220°C for 25-30 min, until crisp.
5. Brown the beef with the spices.
6. Mash the avocados with the lime, onion, coriander and salt.
7. Guacamole, beef and pickled onion on each, lime on the side.
About 30 g protein a taco.
