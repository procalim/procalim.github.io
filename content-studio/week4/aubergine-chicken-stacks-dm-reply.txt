===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Cheesy Baked Eggplant Chicken Buns (makes 4)
Buns: 2 large eggplants (8 rounds, 2 cm thick), 8 slices halal-certified mozzarella (160 g)
Filling: 300 g minced halal chicken breast, 100 g chopped spinach, 100 g ricotta (no gelatin), 1 garlic clove, salt, pepper
Top: 1 beaten egg, 30 g breadcrumbs mixed with 20 g grated halal-certified hard cheese
Tomatoes: 200 g cherry tomatoes, 6 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp olive oil, parsley to finish
1. Salt the rounds for 10 min and pat dry.
2. Cook the chicken until no pink remains, then mix with the spinach, ricotta, garlic, salt and pepper.
3. On 4 rounds: mozzarella, filling, mozzarella, a second round. Press gently.
4. Brush with the egg and cover with the crumb mix.
5. Tomatoes, garlic and oil in a ramekin next to the buns.
6. Bake at 200°C for 30-35 min, until deep golden and the eggplant is soft.
7. Top with the roasted tomatoes and garlic, then parsley.
About 42 g protein a bun.
