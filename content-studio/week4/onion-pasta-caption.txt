Garlic Chicken Pasta Recipe, the whole dinner in one dish 👀
Send this to the friend who hates washing up.

Garlic chicken pasta, baked in one dish: thin onions under paprika chicken and two halved garlic heads, baked until the onions caramelize and the garlic turns soft. Then the chicken is shredded right in the dish with lemon and parsley, the roasted garlic is squeezed in, and fusilli, cream and parmesan go in last. Keep the onions under the chicken: its juices are what caramelize them. Comment PASTA and I'll send you the link to my book of 361 halal recipes.
