===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 2-Ingredient Bagels: no yeast, no boiling, just yogurt and flour.
Ingredients: 1 cup Self-rising flour (or AP flour + 1.5 tsp baking powder + ½ tsp salt), 1 cup Thick Greek Yogurt (must be strained/thick type), 1 Egg (for egg wash), Everything Bagel Seasoning
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Mix flour and yogurt until a dough forms. Knead 2–3 minutes.
3. Divide into 4 balls. Roll each into a rope and join ends to form a bagel.
4. Brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with seasoning.
5. Bake 20–25 minutes until golden brown.
Chef's note: Use FAGE or Chobani full-fat Greek yogurt; watery yogurt ruins the dough.
