===== CAPTION =====
POV: your daughter braided dinner 🌭💙 Save it for the weekend.

Hot dogs (halal beef), mozzarella string cheese, skewers
Batter: 1 cup flour, 2 tbsp sugar, ½ tsp salt, 1 egg, ¾ cup cold milk, 2 tsp baking powder
Crushed blue corn tortilla chips (plain, salted), sugar (for coating)
0. (our braid) Cut a long hot dog lengthwise into 2 strands, top uncut. Add a strand of string cheese and braid all 3. Skewer it.
1. Freeze 20 mins (helps batter stick).
2. Whisk batter ingredients until thick and sticky. Pour into a tall glass.
3. Dip into the batter, coating completely.
4. Roll in the crushed blue chips (press them on).
5. Deep fry at 350°F (175°C) for 3-5 minutes until crisp.
6. Roll in sugar immediately. Top with ketchup and mustard.
