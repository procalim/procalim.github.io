Golden-Seared Herb Short Ribs Recipe | When You're Starving at 3 AM | لما تجوع الساعة ٣ الفجر
Send this to the friend who's always hungry at 3 AM.

Thick bone-in beef short ribs, patted dry and seared until every edge has a deep golden crust, then slow braised with onion, carrot, celery, garlic and a bundle of rosemary, thyme and bay for almost three hours, until the bones slide right out. The braising liquid is strained, reduced with demi-glace and finished with cold butter until it shines like liquid gold. No knife needed.
