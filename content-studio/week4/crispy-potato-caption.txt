Crispy Potato Nests Recipe, the only way your kids forget fries 👀
Send this to the friend who always steals your fries.

Crispy potato nests from 2 potatoes: thin potato sticks coated in a light batter of flour, starch, sparkling mineral water, turmeric, red pepper and salt, then fried in hot oil as loose nests until golden. Serve them with ketchup mixed with mayo, or yogurt on the side. Keep the oil hot and don't skip the starch: that is what keeps them crunchy.
