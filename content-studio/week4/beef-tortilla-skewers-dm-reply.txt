===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Beef Tortilla Skewers with Green Yogurt Sauce (makes 8)
Rolls: 8 large flour tortillas, 800 g lean ground beef (halal), 1 red pepper finely chopped, 1 small onion grated and squeezed dry, a handful of parsley, 1 tbsp tomato paste, 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp black pepper, 1½ tsp salt, 200 g shredded cheddar and mozzarella (halal), 2 tbsp olive oil, 8 long wooden skewers
Sauce: 200 g Greek yogurt (no gelatin), a handful each of coriander and parsley, 1 garlic clove, juice of ½ lemon, ½ green chilli (optional), salt
1. Mix the beef with the pepper, onion, parsley, tomato paste, spices and salt.
2. Trim each tortilla into a square.
3. Spread about 100 g of beef thin to the edges; sprinkle with the cheese.
4. Roll each one up tightly.
5. Push a skewer through each roll, end to end.
6. On a lined tray, brush with the oil; bake at 200°C for 20-25 min until golden and cooked through.
7. Blend the sauce smooth and drizzle it over.
About 34 g protein a skewer.
