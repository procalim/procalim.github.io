===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Viral Date Bark, N° 111 from my book (Sweets from the Book, Ep 1)
Ingredients: 15-20 Medjool dates (pitted), 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter, 1 cup dark chocolate chips (melted), 1/4 cup chopped peanuts or pecans, flaky sea salt
1. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Open the pitted dates and lay them flat, overlapping them slightly to form a solid rectangle.
3. Place another sheet of parchment paper on top and use a rolling pin or the bottom of a heavy cup to smash the dates together into a flat, cohesive "dough" layer.
4. Spread an even layer of peanut butter over the smashed dates.
5. Pour the melted dark chocolate over the peanut butter, spreading it to the edges.
6. Sprinkle with chopped nuts and flaky sea salt.
7. Freeze for 1 hour until solid, then use your hands or a knife to break it into random "bark" shards.
