The Clam and the Pearl 🐚 Korean cream cheese garlic bread with a mozzarella pearl inside. Save it for the weekend.

4 round bread rolls (brioche or soft dinner rolls)
Cream cheese filling: 1 cup cream cheese (softened), 2 tbsp sugar, ¼ cup heavy cream
Garlic butter dip: ½ cup butter (melted), ¼ cup milk, 2 tbsp honey, 3 tbsp garlic (minced), 1 egg, 2 tbsp fresh parsley (chopped)
Our pearls: 4 balls of mozzarella
0. (our clam) Cut each roll level through its side, all around except the back, so the top opens like a shell.
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Cut each roll into 6 wedges, almost all the way down, leaving the base intact so it opens like a flower. (For the clam, the 6 cuts go in the top shell, fanned out from the back.)
3. Whip the cream cheese, sugar, and heavy cream until smooth. Transfer to a piping bag.
4. Pipe the cream cheese generously between every single slice. (Our pearl: a bed of cream cheese inside, a mozzarella ball on it, then close the shell.)
5. Whisk all the garlic butter dip ingredients together in a separate bowl.
6. Dunk each stuffed roll completely into the garlic butter, making sure it gets inside the cuts.
7. Bake for 15–20 minutes until the edges are crispy and the cream cheese is gooey.
Chef's note: leave the base uncut; it is the only thing holding the flower together.
Halal: brioche made without lard, halal-certified cream cheese and mozzarella.
