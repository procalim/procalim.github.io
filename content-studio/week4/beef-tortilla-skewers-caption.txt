Beef tortilla skewers from the oven: seasoned halal beef with red pepper, onion and parsley spread thin on square tortillas, covered in cheddar and mozzarella, rolled tight, skewered and baked at 200°C until golden and crisp. Then a green sauce of Greek yogurt, coriander and parsley over the top. About 34 g of protein a skewer.
