===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Cheesy Beef & Spinach Stuffed Sweet Potato Flatbreads (makes 4)
Dough: 2 medium sweet potatoes (500 g baked flesh), 1 tbsp olive oil, 60 g shredded halal-certified mozzarella, 120 g flour, salt, pepper
Filling: 300 g lean halal ground beef, 1 small red onion, 100 g spinach, 1 tsp paprika, salt, pepper, 80 g shredded halal-certified mozzarella
To cook: 1 tbsp oil; 200 ml tomato sauce for dipping (no wine)
1. Prick the sweet potatoes, rub with the olive oil, bake at 200°C for 45-50 min.
2. Brown the beef with the onion, wilt in the spinach, season.
3. Peel and mash; mix in the 60 g mozzarella, the flour, salt and pepper.
4. Flatten a quarter of the dough on cling film, add the filling and cheese, twist into a ball, unwrap and press into a thick round.
5. Pan-fry 3-4 min a side, until golden.
6. Cut open and dip in the tomato sauce.
About 31 g protein each.
