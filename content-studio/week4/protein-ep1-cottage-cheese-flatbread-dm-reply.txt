===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 The Viral Cottage Cheese Flatbread, Protein from the Book Ep 1
Ingredients: 1 cup full-fat cottage cheese, 2 eggs, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp Italian seasoning, pinch of salt and black pepper
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper (do not use foil, or it will stick).
2. Place the cottage cheese, eggs, and all spices into a blender or food processor.
3. Blend on high for 30 seconds until the mixture is completely smooth and liquid.
4. Pour the batter onto the parchment paper and use a spatula to spread it out into a large, even rectangle.
5. Bake for 35-40 minutes until the edges are golden brown and the flatbread feels firm to the touch.
6. Let it cool completely on the pan before carefully peeling it off the parchment paper. Load it up with your favorite sandwich fillings and roll it like a wrap!
