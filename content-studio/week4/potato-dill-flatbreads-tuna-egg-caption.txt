Potato Dill Flatbreads Recipe | Tuna & Egg | خبز البطاطا بالشبت مع تونة وبيض

Boiled potatoes mashed with flour, mozzarella and fresh dill into a soft dough, pressed into cups with the bottom of a glass and baked until crisp underneath. Then a spicy tuna filling, a fried egg with a runny yolk and a basil leaf on top. About 37 g of protein in every cup.
