===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Crispy Rice Tostadas with Seared Tuna (makes 3)
Tuna: 300 g sushi-grade tuna steak, 3 tbsp halal soy sauce (no alcohol), 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp grated ginger, 4 tbsp white and black sesame
Rice: 150 g sushi rice, cooked, a pinch of salt, 2 tbsp oil
Top: 1 small cucumber, 1 avocado, a handful of edamame, 2 spring onions
Spicy mayo: 3 tbsp light mayo, 1 tbsp sriracha
1. Marinate the tuna for 10 min, then press both sides into the sesame.
2. Sear 45-60 sec a side in a very hot pan, rest 2 min, then slice.
3. Press the warm rice into 3 flat disks with the bottom of a glass.
4. Fry 3-4 min a side until crisp and golden.
5. Top with cucumber, avocado, tuna, edamame and spring onion, then the spicy mayo.
About 33 g protein each. Sushi-grade tuna only; if you're pregnant, sear it through.
