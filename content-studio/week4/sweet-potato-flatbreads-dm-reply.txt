===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Sweet Potato Flatbreads with Seasoned Chicken & Pecans (makes 4)
Flatbreads: 2 medium sweet potatoes (500 g baked flesh), 100 g shredded halal-certified mozzarella, 120 g flour, a pinch of salt
Sauce: 200 g Greek yogurt (no gelatin), 1 garlic clove, juice of ½ lemon, salt
Top: 2 handfuls baby spinach, 4 small halal chicken breasts (500 g), 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt, 1 tbsp oil, 40 g toasted pecans, 20 g grated halal-certified hard cheese
1. Bake the sweet potatoes at 200°C for 45-50 min, peel and mash.
2. Mix in the mozzarella, flour and salt, and knead into a soft dough.
3. Roll 4 balls on a lined tray and press each flat, about 5 mm thick.
4. Bake at 200°C for 18-22 min, until golden in spots.
5. Rub the chicken with the oil and spices, roast at 200°C for 20-22 min, rest.
6. Stir the yogurt with the garlic, lemon and salt.
7. Spread the sauce, then spinach, chicken, pecans and cheese.
About 47 g protein a flatbread.
