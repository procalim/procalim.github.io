===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Viral Cottage Cheese Ice Cream, Protein from the Book Ep 2
Ingredients: 2 cups full-fat cottage cheese, ¼ cup honey or maple syrup, 1 tsp vanilla extract (alcohol-free), mix-ins: strawberry chunks, graham cracker crumbs, or peanut butter & chocolate chips
1. Place cottage cheese, honey, and vanilla extract into a food processor.
2. Blend on high for 2-3 minutes until completely smooth.
3. Pour into the cottage cheese container or a freezer-safe loaf pan.
4. Gently stir in your desired mix-ins.
5. Freeze for 3-4 hours.
6. Let sit on counter for 10 minutes to soften before scooping.
Chef's note: Full-fat cottage cheese is essential; low-fat versions turn icy, not creamy.
