===== ENGLISH, MESSAGE 1 =====
Here's your recipe 🤍 Sweet Potato Steak Bites (makes 4)
Cups: 1 large baked sweet potato (300 g flesh), 120 g flour, 100 g shredded mozzarella, 15 g butter, a pinch of salt
Steak: 300 g halal beef steak in 2 cm cubes, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper
Top: 150 g cherry tomatoes, 1 small red onion, 1 avocado, dill, parsley
Green sauce: 200 g Greek yogurt, a handful of coriander and parsley, 1 garlic clove, juice of ½ lemon, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt
1. Bake the sweet potato at 200°C for 45-50 min, then peel.
2. Mash with the flour, mozzarella, butter and salt, and knead into a soft dough.
3. Roll 4 balls and press the bottom of a glass into each to make a cup.
4. Bake at 200°C for 20-25 min, until golden underneath.
5. Blister the tomatoes, soften the onion, blend the sauce.
6. Sear the steak 1-2 min a side and season.
7. Fill with the steak, tomatoes, onion, herbs and avocado, then pour the sauce.
About 32 g protein a cup.
