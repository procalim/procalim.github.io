Beef Shawarma Recipe | Oven Tower, No Rotisserie | شاورما لحمة بالبيت بلا سيخ دوّار
Send this to someone who owes you a shawarma.

Beef shawarma at home with no rotisserie: thin slices of half-frozen sirloin tossed with yogurt, lemon, garlic and warm spices, stacked into a tower on one skewer and roasted at 220°C until the edges crisp. Shave it, sear it on a hot griddle, roll it in thin Arabic bread with white toum, pickles, tomato and sumac onion, then press it crisp. About 51 g of protein in every wrap.
