# The playbook «طريقنا للفيروسية» (2026-11-21)
The owner, 21 Nov: «الفكرة سارت عنا واداة ig viral موجودة والسكيب حليناها ضايل الإكمال إلى 40% وإذا حلينا المشاركة
والخفظ والمبيعات حتكون موجودة انت كخبير اي خطوة منعملها سجلها».
Every reel goes through the steps below, in order, and each step's check runs before the next. Every step is logged in
the step log at the end: what we did, what came out, the mistake caught or missed, and the rule it added.

## THE DOOR (the formula v2, the owner, 24 Nov: «اعطيني حل»)
Our own reads decide it, not taste. The crunchwrap on the 26 s template: skip 47.3%, 68% gone by 2 s inside the
living-room skit, 21 more points on the prep to 6 s, then flat, and the ones who stayed liked, saved and commented above
normal. The ice cubes (food close at frame one, a sound): 14.5%. The lantern (food first, no person): 22.4%, 35,530.
1. **Frame one:** the food doing something, close, filling the frame, with a sound in 0-0.5 s. His face may be above
   it, eyes on the lens; never a person standing in a room with the food small.
2. **The hook:** yes, one white line in the middle, 6 words or fewer, over 0-2 s. The picture stops the scroll; the line
   adds watch time (the ice cubes: the same skip with and without it, 7.4 s against 5.6 s average watch).
3. **The middle:** moves only (slam, sizzle, lid, pour, throw, landing). No grating, mixing, sprinkling or measuring: it
   cost 20-33 points each time (the crunchwrap, the ice cubes, the lantern).
4. **The end:** the reveal in the last quarter (it opens, it is pulled, it is cut), the cross-section, then the bite
   back where the reel began.
5. **The send:** the send line on screen from the reveal (not the last seconds, where about 5% are left) and in the
   caption. The 3 a.m. crunchwrap (24 Nov): skip 19.6%, average watch 15 s of 30, yet 0 sends and 2 saves from 1,301
   viewers and the views flat after 75 min: the save and send line sits at the reveal, before the average watch runs
   out (by about 45% of the length), and the pinned comment asks for a mention («منشن صاحبك اللي…»), which brings the
   friend in. Every comment answered in the first hour.
6. **The test:** two Trial Reels that differ only in the first second; the lower skip is shared and the next reel starts
   from it. Instagram decides, not us.
7. **Kept from Kevin's motion:** the locked camera, the motion from the action, hard cuts on action, slow motion and
   ramps, the overlap on the shared object, the punch-ins, the match cut. Length 20 s.
First: prompts/w4-marry-me-ring-box-20s-door.txt.

### KNOWING THE AUDIENCE WANTS FOOD AND THE BOOK (the owner, 24 Nov: «كيف بدي أعرف إنه هذا الجمهور مهتم بالطهي والكتاب؟»)

Our takes are half his pace, a third less movement inside each shot, and grey-dark. The cause is in our own prompts:
"slow motion and speed ramps on every throw and drop" and "Shot like a premium food commercial: ... deep shadows".
His prep (grating, squeezing, chopping) is not a cliff: it is fast, 0.5 s a shot, real speed, a sound on every cut.
And his reel is a story: one relatable line («when Germans get hungry at 3 a.m.»), a person at the start and the end
(the couch, then asleep on it), the food in between.
1. **The story line:** frame one carries a relatable situation with an identity, not the recipe's name: «ماما: في أكل
   بالبيت», «لما العرب يجوعوا الساعة 3 الصبح», «لما الضيوف يوصلوا بعد 10 دقايق», «آخر الشهر والبراد فاضي». The
   send line names the person in it (your mum, your 3 a.m. friend).
2. **The pace:** 2 cuts a second, shots of 0.4-0.6 s, hands at real speed; slow motion on one or two hero moments only
   (the eruption, the pull). Prompts ask for it; the edit cuts every shot to its action and speeds the model's slow
   motion back (1.25-1.6x).
3. **The warmth:** warm and saturated (his 137), a lived-in home light, not a black studio.
4. **The person:** at the start and the end, in the story's place.
5. Everything else in THE DOOR stays: food doing something in frame one, the reveal late, two Trial Reels.
