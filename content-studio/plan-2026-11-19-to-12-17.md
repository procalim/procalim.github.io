Comments are covered. Stories reach 3% of the followers and bring no replies. The bio link takes 8 taps out of 1,013
visits.

Instagram on Stories ranking ([Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)):
stories go to followers, ordered by how often the viewer watches the account's stories, how often they interact
with it (likes, DMs) and how close they are. A story reply is a DM.
Trial reels go to non-followers only; followers see them only after "Share to everyone" in the reel's insights
([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first/)). So while every episode is a
trial reel, the stories are the only place the 1,170 followers see the series.

## How long, and when to decide
- Every reel: read at 24 h (skip, the 2 s point, sends per 1,000 reach). A trial reel that beats skip 37% gets
  "Share to everyone" so the followers see it too.
- After 6 episodes on the measured rule (eps 2-7): median skip 37% or less keeps the format; above it, the picture
  and the format change, the sound rule stays.
- 17 Dec: the same 29-day read against the table above (reels reach, profile visits, bio link taps, follows, story
  reach and replies), plus the book sales from the shop's own dashboard (Instagram does not see sales).
