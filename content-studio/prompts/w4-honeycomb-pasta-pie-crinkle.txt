RECIPE (book N° 094, word for word)
Honeycomb Pasta Pie (Rigatoni Cake). A visually stunning, sliceable pasta dish.
INGREDIENTS: 500g Rigatoni pasta (cooked al dente and drained); 2 cups Marinara or Meat sauce; 1 cup Ricotta cheese;
2 cups Mozzarella cheese (shredded); ½ cup Parmesan cheese; Olive oil.
METHOD
01 Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Grease a 9-inch springform pan.
02 Toss cooked rigatoni in olive oil and parmesan so they don't stick.
03 Stand each rigatoni tube upright in the springform pan until tightly packed (it will look like a honeycomb).
04 Pipe a little ricotta into the center of each pasta tube.
05 Pour marinara sauce over the top. Push sauce down into the tubes.
06 Top with a thick layer of mozzarella cheese.
07 Bake 25–30 minutes until cheese is bubbling and golden. Rest 15 minutes before slicing.
(Marinara chosen, as the book allows; halal: no meat, cheeses halal-certified.)
