— Green sauce: 200 g Greek yogurt; coriander and parsley; 1 garlic clove; juice of ½ lemon; ½ green chilli; salt
01 Mix the beef with the pepper, onion, parsley, tomato paste, spices and salt.
02 Trim each tortilla into a square.
03 Spread a thin even layer of the beef to the edges and sprinkle with the cheese.
04 Roll each one up tightly.
05 Lay 4 rolls side by side and push 4 skewers through all 4, evenly spaced. Cut between the skewers.
06 Lay the skewers on a lined tray, brush with the oil and bake at 200°C until golden and the beef is cooked through.
07 Blend the sauce until smooth and drizzle it over the skewers.
