THE RECIPE (for the book; quantities are suggestions for the owner to confirm)
Giant Stuffed Chocolate Banana Pancake
INGREDIENTS
— 1 cup Flour
— 1 tbsp Sugar
— 2 tsp Baking powder
— Pinch of Salt
— 1 Egg
— ¾ cup Milk
— 2 tbsp Melted butter
— ½ tsp Vanilla (alcohol-free)
— The Filling: 2 tbsp Chocolate hazelnut spread, 1 Banana (sliced into coins)
— Chocolate chips (for the top)
— Butter for the pan
METHOD
01 Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Add the egg, milk, melted butter and vanilla and whisk into a thick batter.
02 Butter a small pan over LOW heat and pour in half the batter.
03 Spoon the chocolate spread into the centre and cover it with the banana slices, keeping the edges clear.
04 Pour the rest of the batter over the filling to seal it.
05 Cover and cook 3 minutes until bubbles form, flip, and cook 3 more minutes until golden.
06 Slide onto a plate, sprinkle with chocolate chips and cut while warm.
