RECIPE (follow exactly, every step once, in this order, nothing added):
Jalapeño Popper Smash Burgers
INGREDIENTS
— 1/2 lb Ground Beef (divided into two balls)
— 2 fresh Jalapeños (diced)
— 2 tbsp Cream Cheese
— 2 slices Pepper Jack Cheese
— Burger buns and spicy mayo
METHOD
01 Mix the diced jalapeños and cream cheese together.
02 Press a small indent into your ground beef balls and stuff them with the jalapeño cream cheese mixture. Seal the meat around it.
03 Place the stuffed meatballs onto a screaming hot skillet and smash them down flat with a spatula.
04 Season with salt and pepper. Cook for 2-3 minutes until a crust forms, then flip.
05 Top with Pepper Jack cheese, let it melt, and serve on toasted buns with spicy mayo.
The beef is halal beef, no pork.
