RECIPE (every step once, in this order, nothing added):
High-protein Cookie Dough (Cottage Cheese Hack)
— 1 cup Cottage cheese (full fat); 1 tbsp Maple syrup; 1 tsp Vanilla extract (alcohol-free); 1 cup Almond flour; 1 scoop Vanilla protein powder; 1/4 cup Dark chocolate chips
1 Place the cottage cheese, maple syrup, and vanilla extract into a blender. Blend until absolutely silky smooth.
2 Pour the smooth mixture into a mixing bowl.
3 Stir in the almond flour and protein powder until a thick, dough-like consistency forms.
4 Fold in the chocolate chips.
5 Eat it straight from the bowl with a spoon.
