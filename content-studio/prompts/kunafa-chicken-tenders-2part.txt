THE RECIPE (for the book, quantities to confirm by the owner)
Kunafa-Crusted Soy Garlic Chicken Tenders
INGREDIENTS
— 2 Chicken breasts (sliced into long strips)
— 1 cup Milk + 1 tbsp Minced garlic + 1 tbsp Korean red chili powder (gochugaru) + 1 tsp Salt (marinade)
— 1 cup Flour + ¾ cup Cold water (batter)
— 250 g Kataifi (shredded kunafa dough), cut short
— Oil for deep frying
— The Glaze: 4 tbsp Soy sauce, 4 tbsp Corn syrup or honey, 1 tbsp Minced garlic
— Chopped green onions
METHOD
01 Slice the chicken breasts lengthwise into long strips.
02 Marinate the strips in the milk, garlic, chili powder and salt for 30 minutes.
03 Whisk the flour and cold water into a thick batter.
04 Dip each strip in the batter, then press it into the kataifi until fully covered.
05 Deep fry at 340°F (170°C) for 5–6 minutes until golden and very crispy.
06 Simmer the soy sauce, syrup and garlic until it bubbles and turns thick and glossy.
07 Dip the hot strips in the glaze and top with chopped green onions.
