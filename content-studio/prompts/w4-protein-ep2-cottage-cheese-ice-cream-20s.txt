— 2 cups Full-fat Cottage Cheese; ¼ cup Honey; 1 tsp Vanilla extract (alcohol-free); Mix-ins: Strawberry chunks

2 Blend on high for 2–3 minutes until completely smooth.
3 Pour into the cottage cheese container.
4 Gently stir in your desired mix-ins.
5 Freeze for 3–4 hours.
6 Let sit on counter for 10 minutes to soften before scooping.
