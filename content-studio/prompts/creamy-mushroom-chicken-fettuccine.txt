THE RECIPE (for the book; quantities are suggestions for the owner to confirm)
Creamy One-Pan Mushroom Chicken Fettuccine
INGREDIENTS
— 2 Thin chicken breast cutlets
— The Rub: 1 tbsp Olive oil, 1 tsp Paprika, 1 tsp Garlic powder, ½ tsp Chili flakes, 1 tsp Dried Italian herbs, Salt, Pepper
— 2 tbsp Butter
— 1 Shallot (finely chopped) + 2 cloves Garlic (minced)
— 200 g White mushrooms (sliced)
— 1½ cups Chicken broth (halal)
— 1 cup Cooking cream
— ½ cup Grated parmesan (halal-certified)
— 1 tsp Italian seasoning + ½ tsp Chili flakes + Black pepper
— 250 g Fettuccine nests (uncooked)
— Chopped parsley
METHOD
01 Rub the chicken with the oil and spices.
02 Melt the butter in a large pan and sear the chicken 4–5 minutes per side until golden. Set aside.
03 In the same pan, soften the shallot and garlic in the drippings.
04 Add the mushrooms and cook until golden.
05 Pour in the broth and cream, then add the parmesan and seasoning.
06 Lay in the dry fettuccine nests and simmer 10–12 minutes, stirring, until the pasta is tender and the sauce is creamy.
07 Slice the chicken, lay it on top and finish with parsley.
