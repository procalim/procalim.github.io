The "Dense Bean Salad"
— 1 can Garbanzo beans (Chickpeas), rinsed; 1 can Cannellini beans (White beans), rinsed; 1 cup Cherry tomatoes (halved); 1 Bell pepper (diced); 1/2 Red onion (finely diced); 1/2 cup Feta cheese (crumbled); 1/4 cup halal beef salami (diced small); Fresh parsley and basil (chopped); Dressing: 1/3 cup Olive oil, 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 clove garlic (minced), Salt, Pepper, Oregano
1 In a massive bowl, combine the beans, tomatoes, pepper, onion, cheese, meats, and herbs.
2 In a small jar, vigorously shake the dressing ingredients together until emulsified.

4 Let it marinate in the fridge for at least 30 minutes before eating. The longer it sits, the better it tastes!
