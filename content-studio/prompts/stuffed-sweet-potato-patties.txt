THE RECIPE (for the book; quantities are suggestions for the owner to confirm)
Cheesy Beef & Spinach Stuffed Sweet Potato Patties
INGREDIENTS
— 2 Medium sweet potatoes
— 1 tbsp Olive oil
— 1 cup Shredded mozzarella (½ for the dough, ½ for the filling; halal-certified)
— ½ cup Flour
— Salt and Pepper
— The Filling: 250 g Ground beef (halal), 1 small Red onion (chopped), 2 cups Fresh spinach, 1 tsp Paprika, Salt, Pepper
— Oil for the pan
— Tomato sauce for dipping
METHOD
01 Prick the sweet potatoes, rub with the olive oil and bake at 400°F (200°C) for 45–50 minutes until soft.
02 Brown the beef with the onion, stir in the spinach until it wilts, and season with paprika, salt and pepper.
03 Peel and mash the sweet potatoes, then mix in half the mozzarella, the flour, salt and pepper into a soft dough.
04 Put a scoop of dough on plastic wrap, flatten it, add the filling and mozzarella, gather the wrap and twist it into a ball, then unwrap and press into a thick patty.
05 Pan-fry in a little oil for 3–4 minutes per side until golden.
06 Cut open and serve with tomato sauce.
