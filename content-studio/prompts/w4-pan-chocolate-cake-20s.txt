RECIPE (follow exactly, every step once, in this order, nothing added):
Pan Chocolate Cake, no oven
INGREDIENTS
— Cake: 3 Eggs, 1 cup Sugar, ½ cup Milk, ½ cup Vegetable oil, 3 tbsp Cocoa powder, 1½ cups Flour, 1 sachet Baking powder, pinch of Salt, ½ cup Hot coffee
— Sauce: 1 cup Milk, ⅓ cup Sugar, 2 tbsp Cocoa powder, 2 tbsp Vegetable oil, 50 g Dark chocolate
METHOD
01 Crack the eggs into the cold pan, add the sugar and whisk until pale.
02 Whisk in the milk and the oil.
03 Add the cocoa and whisk until smooth.
04 Add the flour, baking powder and salt and whisk until smooth.
05 Pour in the hot coffee and whisk to a thin, glossy batter.
06 Cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15–20 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
07 Poke holes all over the hot cake.
08 Simmer the sauce for 2–3 minutes until glossy, pour it over the hot cake and spread it to the edges.
09 Rest 30 minutes, then cut into squares.
CHEF'S NOTE: Pour the sauce while the cake is hot: it drinks it all.
