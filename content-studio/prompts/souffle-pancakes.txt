RECIPE (follow exactly, every step once, in this order, nothing added):
Japanese Souffle Pancakes
INGREDIENTS
- 2 Eggs (separated)
- 2 tbsp Milk
- 1/2 tsp Vanilla extract
- 3 tbsp Cake flour (or All-purpose flour)
- 1/4 tsp Baking powder
- 2 tbsp Sugar
- Oil for greasing
METHOD
1. Mix egg yolks, milk, and vanilla. Sift in flour and baking powder; mix until smooth.
2. In a separate clean bowl, whip egg whites. Gradually add sugar until stiff glossy peaks form.
3. Gently fold meringue into yolk mixture in 3 batches. Do not overmix.
4. Heat non-stick pan over LOW heat. Lightly grease.
5. Scoop tall mounds of batter. Add 1 tsp water to empty pan spaces and cover with lid (to steam).
6. Cook 4-5 minutes, flip gently, cook another 4-5 minutes covered.
7. Serve immediately with powdered sugar and syrup.
CHEF'S NOTE: LOW heat is the secret, these pancakes steam as much as they cook.
