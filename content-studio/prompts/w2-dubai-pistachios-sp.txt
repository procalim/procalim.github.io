RECIPE (follow exactly, every step once, in this order, nothing added):
The Viral Dubai Pistachio Kunafa Chocolate
INGREDIENTS
— 300g High-quality Milk or Dark Chocolate (chopped)
— 1 cup Kataifi pastry (shredded phyllo dough, chopped small)
— 2 tbsp Butter
— ½ cup Pistachio cream (sweetened)
— 1 tbsp Tahini (optional)
— Silicone mold with 3 giant pistachio-shaped cavities (the new shape)
METHOD
01 Melt 150g chocolate. Pour into silicone mold, brushing up sides to create a thick shell. Chill 20 minutes.
02 In a skillet, melt butter over medium heat. Add chopped Kataifi pastry.
03 Toast stirring constantly until deep golden brown and incredibly crunchy (5–7 minutes). Let cool.
04 Mix crispy Kataifi with pistachio cream and tahini until coated but still crunchy.
05 Remove chocolate mold from fridge. Pack pistachio filling tightly into the chocolate shell.
06 Melt remaining 150g chocolate and pour over filling to seal. Smooth with a spatula.
07 Refrigerate 1 hour until completely set. Pop out of mold and snap in half to reveal the bright green center.
CHEF'S NOTE: Toast the Kataifi until it's DEEPLY golden — the crunch is the whole point.
