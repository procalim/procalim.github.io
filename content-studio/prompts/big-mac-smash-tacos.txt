RECIPE (follow exactly, every step once, in this order, nothing added):
Big Mac Smash Tacos
INGREDIENTS
- Small flour tortillas
- Ground beef (minced)
- Cheddar cheese slices
- Shredded lettuce, Diced onions, Pickles
- Sauce: 1/2 cup Mayo, 2 tbsp Ketchup, 1 tbsp Mustard
- 1 tsp Pickle juice, Paprika, Garlic powder
METHOD
01 Mix the sauce ingredients in a small bowl.
02 Press a ball of ground beef flat onto a tortilla. Season with salt and pepper.
03 Heat a skillet with oil. Place tortilla meat-side down.
04 Cook 3-4 minutes until meat is browned and crispy. Flip.
05 Place cheese on the meat side. Cover 1 minute to melt.
06 Top with lettuce, onions, pickles, and special sauce. Fold and eat.
CHEF'S NOTE
Use extra-lean beef for crispier results without excess grease.
