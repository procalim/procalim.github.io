— 4 thin round Arabic flatbreads; cucumber pickles; tomatoes; red onion with sumac and parsley
01 Slice the half-frozen beef as thin as you can.
02 Toss it with the yogurt, lemon, vinegar, garlic, oil, spices and salt.
03 Stand the skewer upright in the onion half on a tray and stack the slices tightly onto it into a tower.

06 Sear the shavings on a smoking-hot cast-iron griddle until crisp.
