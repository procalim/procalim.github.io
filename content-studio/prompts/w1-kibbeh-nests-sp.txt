Kibbeh Nests
INGREDIENTS
— 1½ cups Fine Bulgur
— 450g Lean Ground Lamb or Beef (shell)
— Onion
— Allspice
— Salt
— Pepper | Filling: Ground Lamb
— Pine Nuts
— Allspice
— Olive Oil
METHOD
01 Process shell ingredients to a fine paste.
02 Saute filling ingredients until browned.
03 Form balls: flatten shell mixture on plastic wrap, stuff with filling, twist the wrap to seal. Unwrap, press the remaining shell mixture through a potato ricer and roll each ball in the strands until fully covered.
04 Deep-fry at 350°F (175°C) until deep brown and cooked through.
All meat is halal lamb or beef, no pork.
