Gochujang Caramel Fried Chicken
INGREDIENTS
— 1 lb Boneless Chicken Thighs (cut into chunks)
— 1 cup Cornstarch
— Oil for frying
— The Caramel: 3 tbsp Gochujang (Korean chili paste), 2 tbsp Honey, 2 tbsp Brown sugar, 1 tbsp Soy sauce, 1 tbsp Water.
01 Toss the chicken chunks in the cornstarch until heavily coated.
02 Deep fry or shallow fry at 375°F (190°C) for 6–8 minutes until super crispy and golden. Drain on a wire rack.
03 In a separate large wok or skillet, combine the gochujang, honey, brown sugar, soy sauce, and water.
04 Simmer the sauce over medium heat until it bubbles vigorously and thickens into a sticky syrup.
05 Toss the crispy chicken in the bubbling caramel off the heat until every piece shines. Garnish with sesame seeds.
