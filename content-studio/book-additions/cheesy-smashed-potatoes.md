## N° 015 Smashed Potatoes, word for word
Small baby potatoes (skin on); olive oil; melted butter; garlic powder, rosemary, salt, pepper.
01 Boil potatoes in salted water until fork-tender (15–20 mins). Drain and steam dry.
02 Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
03 Place potatoes on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
04 Press down on each potato with a glass to smash flat (keep in one piece).
05 Brush generously with olive oil, melted butter, and spices.
06 Bake 30–40 minutes until deep golden brown and super crispy.
Tip: Letting the potatoes steam-dry after boiling is key to maximum crispiness.

## What the reel adds
— 100 g shredded cheese (halal-certified): a pinch on the paper under each potato before step 03, and more on top after step 05.
— A pinch of paprika with the spices.
— The dip: 150 g thick yogurt, 1 small garlic clove crushed, salt and dried herbs.
Kitchen test owed: the cheese under the potato crisping without burning at 220°C.
