# Crispy Mashed Potato Flatbread Tacos with Beef & Guacamole (new recipe for the new PDF; outside-account plan, 17 Nov 3 pm)

## Ingredients
The flatbreads
- 600 g potatoes, boiled and peeled
- 80 g flour
- 60 g shredded mozzarella (halal-certified)
- ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil

The beef
- 400 g lean ground beef (halal)
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp chilli powder, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil

The guacamole
- 2 ripe avocados, juice of 1 lime, ¼ red onion finely chopped, a handful of coriander, salt

Quick pickled onions
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced; 60 ml white vinegar (not wine vinegar), 60 ml water, 1 tsp sugar, ½ tsp salt

Lime wedges to serve

## Method
1. Cover the sliced onion with the vinegar, water, sugar and salt and leave it for 15 minutes.
2. Mash the warm potatoes with the flour, mozzarella, garlic powder and salt into a soft dough.
3. Roll 4 balls on a lined tray, drizzle with the oil and press each flat with your palm, about 5 mm thick, leaving finger ridges.
4. Bake at 220°C for 25-30 minutes, until deep golden and crisp at the edges.
5. Brown the beef in the oil with the spices and salt.
6. Mash the avocados with the lime, onion, coriander and salt.
7. Spread the guacamole on the crisp rounds, top with the beef and the pickled onions, and serve with lime.

## Per taco (estimate)
About 560 kcal, 30 g protein, 52 g carbs, 26 g fat.

## Halal
- Halal beef.
- Mozzarella halal-certified.
- White vinegar, never wine vinegar.
