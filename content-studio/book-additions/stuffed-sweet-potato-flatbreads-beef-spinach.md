# Cheesy Beef & Spinach Stuffed Sweet Potato Flatbreads (outside-account plan, 17 Nov 10 pm)
Source: fiteat.rima's reel DeXzoDtpSVS (6.17M plays, 9,901 comments). The same reel our stuffed patties copied on 27 Oct
(36,392 views, our best in 30 days); the recipe is the one written then (prompts/stuffed-sweet-potato-patties.txt), in

## Ingredients
The dough
- 2 medium sweet potatoes (about 500 g flesh once baked), 1 tbsp olive oil
- 60 g shredded mozzarella (halal-certified)
- 120 g flour
- Salt and pepper

The filling
- 300 g lean ground beef (halal)
- 1 small red onion, chopped
- 100 g fresh spinach
- 1 tsp paprika, salt, pepper
- 80 g shredded mozzarella (halal-certified)

To cook and serve
- 1 tbsp oil for the pan
- 200 ml tomato sauce for dipping (passata simmered with garlic; no wine)

## Method
1. Prick the sweet potatoes, rub with the olive oil and bake at 200°C for 45-50 minutes, until soft.
2. Brown the beef with the onion, stir in the spinach until it wilts, and season with the paprika, salt and pepper.
3. Peel and mash the sweet potatoes, then mix in the 60 g mozzarella, the flour, salt and pepper into a soft dough.
4. Put a quarter of the dough on cling film and flatten it. Add a quarter of the filling and of the 80 g mozzarella, gather the film and twist it into a ball, then unwrap and press into a thick round.
5. Pan-fry in the oil for 3-4 minutes a side, until golden.
6. Cut open and serve with the tomato sauce.

## Per flatbread (estimate)
About 520 kcal, 31 g protein, 56 g carbs, 19 g fat.

## Halal
- Halal beef.
- Mozzarella halal-certified.
- The tomato sauce has no wine.
