## N° 008 The Tortilla Wrap Hack (p154), word for word
A revolutionary folding technique for sandwiches.
INGREDIENTS
— 1 Large flour tortilla
— Zone 1: Burger sauce or Mayo
— Zone 2: Grilled Chicken or Cold cuts
— Zone 3: Lettuce and Tomato
— Zone 4: Cheese slices
METHOD
01 Lay tortilla flat. Make one cut from center down to the bottom edge.
02 Divide tortilla visually into 4 quadrants. Place one ingredient per quadrant.
03 Fold bottom-left quadrant up over top-left.
04 Fold that section over to top-right.
05 Fold that section down to bottom-right. You now have a triangle.
06 Toast in a panini press or skillet until golden and cheese melts.
CHEF'S NOTE
Place the cheese in the quadrant that ends up on the outside so it melts against the pan

## N° 110 McDonald's Hashbrown Hack (Waffle Iron) (p258), word for word
INGREDIENTS
— 2 large Russet potatoes (peeled)
— 1 Egg
— 1 tbsp Cornstarch
— 1 tsp Onion powder
— Salt and Black pepper
— Oil spray
METHOD
01 Getting that perfect fast-food crunch without deep frying.
02 Grate the potatoes using a box grater.
03 Place the grated potatoes in a clean kitchen towel and squeeze as hard as you can to remove all the liquid (dry
   potatoes equal crispy hashbrowns).
04 In a bowl, mix the dry grated potatoes with the egg, cornstarch, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
05 Preheat a waffle iron to high heat and spray generously with oil.
06 Place a handful of the potato mixture into the waffle iron, close the lid, and cook for 5–8 minutes until deeply
   golden brown and crunchy.

## Ours
Zone 2 (the grilled chicken) gets the waffle hashbrown on top before the folds; Zone 4 (the cheese) ends on the
outside, as the chef's note says.
