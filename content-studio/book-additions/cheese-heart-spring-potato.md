## Why this family (the lantern as the reference)
- **The lantern's grammar:** a food everyone knows opens in one push into a shape nobody has seen, with the cheese
  visible inside. Here one pull opens the potato into a spring, and a white disc of cheese sits inside every coil.
- **Frame one has sharp hits:** the corer's pop, the cheese's squeak, then 8 or more knife cuts between the chopsticks
  in under 2 s, then the pull. The lantern had 6 snaps in 0-3 s.
- **The potato travels:** cheap and known in every country of the lantern's audience (Thailand, Indonesia, the US,
  Brazil, Mexico). On this account, potato dishes were the best senders (sweet potato patties 221 sends, sweet
  potato flatbreads 42 shares).
- **Our logs:** no accordion, tornado or spiral potato was made before (generation-log, hook-log, ideas, carousels).

## The book, word for word (N° 026, p173)
Accordion Potatoes
Potatoes cut to stretch like an accordion — a visual masterpiece.

INGREDIENTS
— Large oblong Potatoes (peeled or unpeeled)
— 2 Chopsticks (or skewers)
— Melted Butter + Garlic powder + Parsley
— Skewers (for holding shape)

METHOD
01 Place a potato between two chopsticks (prevents cutting all the way through).
02 Side 1: Make vertical straight cuts all along the potato.
03 Side 2: Flip the potato. Make diagonal cuts (45-degree angle) all along.
04 Gently pull ends; the potato should stretch open like a slinky.
05 Push a skewer through the center to hold it stretched.
06 Brush with garlic butter getting into all grooves.
07 Air Fry at 375°F (190°C) for 15–20 minutes until crispy.

CHEF'S NOTE
Go slowly with the cuts — even spacing makes the accordion effect more dramatic

## The addition: the cheese heart (before step 01; for the owner to approve and test)
00 Push an apple corer through the potato from end to end and take out the core. Push a frozen mozzarella stick into
   the tunnel. Then the book's steps 01-07: the cuts go through the cheese too, so a disc of cheese stays inside
   every coil, and the skewer in step 05 goes through the cheese.
Halal: halal-certified mozzarella; butter (no lard); nothing else to change.
