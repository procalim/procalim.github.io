# N° 365 The Salad Tower «برج السلطة» (2026-11-25, a new recipe for the book)
Written from the reference the owner chose (a chef's plated salad tower, 19.9 s; edits/salad-tower/), for the owner's
approval and a kitchen test before it goes into the book. Halal: feta halal-certified (microbial rennet).

A ring-pressed tower of sliced beetroot, a crisp lemony salad, avocado and feta, crowned with a nest of julienne vegetables
on a plate flicked with three sauces.
Serves 1.

INGREDIENTS
The tower
— 2 cooked beetroots, thinly sliced
— 1 cup iceberg lettuce, finely shredded
— 1 small carrot, cut in thin strips
— ¼ red pepper, cut in thin strips
— 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp lemon juice, salt and pepper
— ½ avocado, thinly sliced
— 50 g feta (halal-certified), in 1.5 cm cubes, a pinch of black pepper
The crown
— 1 carrot and 1 small yellow courgette, cut in long thin strips; 2 green onions, the green ends
The plate
— 2 tbsp beetroot purée, 1 tbsp carrot purée, 1 tbsp parsley oil (blended parsley and olive oil)

METHOD
01 Hold a spoon of each purée over the plate and tap it against the edge of a second plate, so it flicks out in streaks.
02 Set a 10 cm ring in the centre and press the beetroot slices into it in layers.
03 Toss the lettuce, carrot and pepper with the oil, lemon, salt and pepper; fill the ring and press firmly.
04 Fan the avocado over the top and set the feta cubes round it; pepper the feta.
05 Pile the crown of julienne strips and green onion on top.
06 Lift the ring straight up, slowly.

CHEF'S NOTE
Press every layer firmly: a pressed tower stands on its own when the ring comes off.

Kitchen test owed: the ring size against the amounts; whether the salad holds without the ring.
