# N° 365 The Salad Tower «برج السلطة» (2026-11-25, a new recipe for the book)
Written from the owner's Genjutsu video (job 5bbfcf0c, him in his kitchen, 20 s), for the owner's approval and a kitchen
test before it goes into the book. Genjutsu changed the reference's food: a wall of avocado slices with tomato in place
of the beetroot base, rocket in place of the shredded salad, crispy potato straws in place of the julienne crown; this
recipe follows the reel, not the reference (25 Nov). Halal: feta halal-certified (microbial rennet).

A ring-pressed tower of avocado, tomato and lemony rocket, topped with an avocado fan, feta and a crown of crispy potato
straws, on a plate flicked with beetroot and herb oil.
Serves 1.

INGREDIENTS
The tower
— 1 avocado, thinly sliced
— 1 tomato, diced
— 2 handfuls of rocket (arugula)
— 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp lemon juice, salt and pepper
— 50 g feta (halal-certified), in 1.5 cm cubes
The crown
— 1 small potato, cut in very thin straws, rinsed and dried; oil for frying; salt
The plate
— 2 tbsp beetroot purée, 1 tbsp parsley oil (blended parsley and olive oil)

METHOD
01 Fry the potato straws in hot oil until golden and crisp, drain and salt.
02 Hold a spoon of each purée over the plate and tap it against the edge of a second plate, so it flicks out in streaks.
03 Set a 10 cm ring in the centre, line its wall with avocado slices and put the tomato in the bottom.
04 Toss the rocket with the oil, lemon, salt and pepper; fill the ring and press firmly.
05 Fan the remaining avocado on top and set the feta cubes round it.
06 Pile the potato straws on top, then lift the ring straight up, slowly.

CHEF'S NOTE
Press the rocket firmly: a pressed tower stands on its own when the ring comes off.

Kitchen test owed: the ring size against the amounts; whether the rocket holds without the ring.
