# Crunchy Chocolate Chickpea Bark - reference recipe from the owner (2026-11-12)
Sent by the owner in chat with a photo of the clusters in a white ramekin. Not in the book: it goes into the book (and a
new PDF) only after the owner approves it. Copied word for word:

Crunchy Chocolate Chickpea Bark
3 Ingredient Snacks, 5 Minute Snacks, Chocolatey Snack, Healthy Snacks, High Protein Snacks, Vegan Snacks
PREP  Prep Time: 5 minutes
FREEZE  15 minutes
SERVES  3
Ingredients
1 can chickpeas
1 tbsp sugar/sweetener
(optional) 1 tbsp oil
2/3 cup chocolate chips
Instructions
Step 1 Drain the chickpeas and rinse
Step 2 Lay it out on a piece of parchment paper
Step 3 Mix the chickpeas with oil and sugar
Step 4 Air fry @450F for 15-20 minutes
Step 5 Melt the chocolate chips and mix into the chickpeas
Step 6 Lay out the chickpeas on a piece of parchment paper and freeze for 1 hour, break apart and enjoy!

## For the owner to confirm
- The freeze time: 15 minutes in the header, 1 hour in step 6. Captions use neither until he picks one.
- 450°F is 230°C; many air fryers stop at 200°C (400°F).

## Halal
Chickpeas, sugar, oil, chocolate chips (a brand with no alcohol-based flavouring). Nothing to change.
