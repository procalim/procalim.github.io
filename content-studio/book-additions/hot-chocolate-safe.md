## The book, word for word (N° 027, p174; read from the owner's book PDF, 21 Nov)
Hot Chocolate Bombs
Chocolate spheres that 'explode' with marshmallows when hot milk is poured over them.

INGREDIENTS
— Semi-sweet Chocolate chips (melted)
— Hot Cocoa mix (powder)
— Mini Marshmallows
— Silicone sphere mold

METHOD
01 Coat inside of silicone molds with melted chocolate. Freeze 5 mins. Do a second coat.
02 Gently pop chocolate half-spheres out of mold.
03 Fill half the spheres with 1 tbsp cocoa powder and a handful of marshmallows.
04 Warm a plate in the microwave. Rub empty half-sphere edges on the plate to melt slightly.
05 Stick the empty half onto a filled half to seal into a ball.
06 To Serve: Place bomb in a mug. Pour steaming hot milk over it.

CHEF'S NOTE
Do two layers of chocolate coating for a bomb that doesn't crack

## The addition: the safe (for the owner to approve and test)
- A silicone mould of a small cube safe, in two halves, in place of the sphere mould: the front half carries the door,
  the dial, the handle and the rivets; the back half is the box. Steps 01-06 as written, on the two halves.
- With the cocoa and the marshmallows in step 03: a few small round golden cookies, the coins. They rattle when the
  sealed safe is shaken.
Halal: gelatin-free mini marshmallows only; chocolate chips, cocoa mix and cookies checked for gelatin and alcohol-based
flavourings.
