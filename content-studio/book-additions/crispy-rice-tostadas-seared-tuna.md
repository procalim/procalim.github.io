# Crispy Rice Tostadas with Seared Tuna (new recipe for the new PDF; outside-account plan, 14 Nov 10 pm)

quantities are ours. UNTESTED: starting points. Macros are estimates. Makes 3.

## Ingredients
- 300 g sushi-grade tuna steak (one thick piece)
- 3 tbsp halal-certified soy sauce (no added alcohol), 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp grated ginger
- 4 tbsp white and black sesame seeds
- 150 g sushi rice (dry), cooked, with a pinch of salt
- 2 tbsp oil for the pan
- 1 small cucumber, 1 avocado, a handful of shelled edamame, 2 spring onions
- Spicy mayo: 3 tbsp light mayonnaise and 1 tbsp sriracha

## Method
1. Marinate the tuna in the soy sauce, sesame oil and ginger for 10 minutes, turning once.
2. Press both sides into the sesame seeds.
3. Sear in a very hot pan for 45-60 seconds a side. Rest it for 2 minutes, then slice 1 cm thick.
4. Spoon 3 mounds of warm rice onto baking paper and press each flat with the bottom of a glass, about 1 cm thick.
5. Fry the disks in the oil for 3-4 minutes a side, until crisp and golden.
6. Top with cucumber, avocado and the tuna, then the edamame and spring onion. Drizzle the spicy mayo.

## Per tostada (estimate)
About 520 kcal, 33 g protein, 50 g carbs, 22 g fat.

## Halal and safety
- Soy sauce halal-certified with no added alcohol; no mirin.
- Seared tuna stays rare inside, so use sushi-grade fish only. For anyone pregnant, sear it through.
