# Baked Sweet Potato Flatbreads with Seasoned Chicken & Pecans (new recipe for the new PDF; outside-account plan, 16 Nov 10 pm)
Source: fiteat.rima's reel DesCH2uuD2L (6.9M plays), read frame by frame 2026-11-14, written in our own words. The
quantities are ours. UNTESTED: starting points. Macros are estimates. Makes 4 flatbreads.

## Ingredients
The flatbreads
- 2 medium sweet potatoes (about 500 g flesh once baked)
- 100 g shredded mozzarella (halal-certified)
- 120 g flour
- A pinch of salt

The top
- 200 g Greek yogurt (no gelatin), 1 garlic clove, juice of ½ lemon, salt
- 2 handfuls of baby spinach
- 4 small chicken breasts (about 500 g, halal), 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt, 1 tbsp oil
- 40 g pecans, toasted and chopped
- 20 g grated hard cheese (halal-certified)

## Method
1. Bake the sweet potatoes at 200°C for 45-50 minutes. Peel them and mash the flesh.
2. Mix in the mozzarella, flour and salt, and knead into a soft dough.
3. Roll 4 balls on a lined tray and press each flat with your palm, about 5 mm thick.
4. Bake at 200°C for 18-22 minutes, until golden-brown in spots.
5. Rub the chicken with the oil and spices. Roast at 200°C for 20-22 minutes, until cooked through, then rest it.
6. Stir the yogurt with the garlic, lemon and salt.
7. Spread the sauce on each flatbread. Top with spinach, a chicken breast, pecans and cheese.

## Per flatbread (estimate)
About 600 kcal, 47 g protein, 60 g carbs, 18 g fat.

## Halal
- Halal chicken.
- Cheeses halal-certified.
- Yogurt with no gelatin.
