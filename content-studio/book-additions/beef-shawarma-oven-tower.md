# Beef Shawarma, the Oven Tower (new recipe for the new PDF; owner's request 2026-11-15, the cookcut 20 s reel)
Not in the book: the English book has no shawarma (searched 2026-11-15; it has kibbeh, maqluba and the Dubai kunafa).
Written as a new recipe for the owner's approval before generation. UNTESTED: starting points. Macros are estimates.
Makes 4 wraps.

## Ingredients
The beef
- 800 g beef sirloin or rump (halal), half-frozen so it slices thin
- 100 g plain yogurt (no gelatin)
- Juice of 1 lemon and 1 tbsp white vinegar (not wine vinegar)
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp each cumin, paprika and ground coriander; ½ tsp each cinnamon, cardamom, allspice and black pepper; 1½ tsp salt
- ½ onion (the base of the tower) and 1 long steel skewer

Toum (garlic sauce)
- 6 garlic cloves, ½ tsp salt, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 100 ml neutral oil, 2 tbsp ice water

To serve
- 4 thin round Arabic flatbreads
- Cucumber pickles, 2 tomatoes sliced, 1 red onion sliced with 1 tsp sumac and chopped parsley

## Method
1. Slice the half-frozen beef as thin as you can.
2. Toss it with the yogurt, lemon, vinegar, garlic, oil, spices and salt; marinate at least 2 hours.
3. Stand the skewer upright in the onion half on a lined tray and stack the slices tightly onto it into a tower.
4. Roast at 220°C for 45-50 minutes, until dark and crisp at the edges.
5. Shave thin slices down the side of the tower with a long sharp knife.
6. Sear the shavings on a smoking-hot cast-iron griddle for 1-2 minutes, until crisp.
7. Toum: blend the garlic, salt and lemon, then stream in the oil and the ice water until thick and white.
8. Warm the bread on the griddle, spread the toum, add the pickles, tomato and sumac onion, pile on the beef and roll tight.
9. Press the roll on the griddle until crisp, then cut it in half.

## Per wrap (estimate)
About 800 kcal, 51 g protein, 42 g carbs, 47 g fat.

## Halal
- Halal beef.
- Yogurt with no gelatin.
- White vinegar, never wine vinegar; no alcohol, no pork.
