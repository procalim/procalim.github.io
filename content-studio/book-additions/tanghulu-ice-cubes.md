## The book, word for word (N° 020, p167; the owner's book PDF)
Tanghulu (Microwave Method)
Candied fruit with a glass-like crunchy sugar shell.

INGREDIENTS
— Strawberries or Grapes (washed and completely dried)
— 1 cup White Sugar
— ½ cup Water
— Bamboo skewers

METHOD
01 Skewer the fruit. Critical: Fruit must be 100% dry or sugar won't stick.
02 In a microwave-safe mug, mix sugar and water. Do NOT stir after mixing.
03 Microwave in 30-second intervals. Usually takes 5–8 minutes total.
04 Test: Dip a chopstick in sugar then in ice water. If it crunches, it's ready.
05 Working fast, dip fruit into hot sugar syrup and immediately plunge into ice water to set shell.

CHEF'S NOTE
Work extremely fast — the sugar sets within seconds of touching the fruit

## The addition: the ice-cube tray (for the owner to approve and test)
01 (ours) In place of the skewers, set one completely dry grape in each cell of a silicone ice-cube tray.
05 (ours) Working fast, pour the hot syrup over the grapes to fill each cell, and immediately set the tray in ice water.
   Pop the cubes out when they are hard.
Halal: nothing to change.
