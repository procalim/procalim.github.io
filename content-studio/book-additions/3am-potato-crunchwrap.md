# N° 362 The 3 a.m. Potato Crunchwrap «كرانش راب البطاطا تبع الساعة 3» (2026-11-24, a new recipe for the book)
The owner, 24 Nov: «الوصفة بتنضاف على الكتاب. يعني الكتاب بدل ما يكون 361 وصفة بصير 362 وصفة». The reel: the owner's
motion-control video (Higgsfield, job 236a77db) on the German's 60M reel; this page is the recipe the video shows,
written from its frames, for the owner's approval and a kitchen test before it goes into the book.
Halal: the mince in the video is meatless (walnuts); halal beef mince is the swap. Soy sauce halal-certified (many hold
alcohol), cheese halal-certified, sour cream or thick yogurt with no gelatin.

A golden potato pancake folded with a sticky walnut mince, cheese and cool toppings inside a toasted tortilla parcel.
Makes 1 big crunchwrap (2 halves).

INGREDIENTS
The potato pancake
— 2 large potatoes (peeled)
— ½ tsp salt
— 2 tbsp oil
The mince
— 100 g walnuts, finely chopped (or 250 g halal beef mince)
— 2 garlic cloves, crushed
— 1 tbsp oil
— 1 tbsp soy sauce (halal-certified)
— 1 tbsp tomato paste
— 1 tsp smoked paprika
— Salt and black pepper
The wrap
— 1 large flour tortilla and 1 small flour tortilla
— 50 g grated cheese (halal-certified)
— 2 tbsp sour cream or thick yogurt
— A handful of shredded lettuce
— 1 tomato, diced
— 2 green onions, chopped

METHOD
01 Grate the potatoes on a box grater. Wrap them in a clean towel and squeeze out all the liquid.
02 Season with the salt, press flat into a hot oiled pan the size of the small tortilla, and fry 4-5 minutes a side
   until golden and crisp.
03 Fry the garlic and the walnuts in the oil for 3 minutes. Add the soy sauce, tomato paste and paprika, season, and
   cook 2 minutes until dark and sticky.
04 Lay the large tortilla flat. In the centre, in this order: the cheese, the mince, the potato pancake, the sour cream,
   the lettuce, the tomato and the green onions. Cover with the small tortilla.
05 Fold the edges of the large tortilla up over the small one in pleats, all the way round, into a sealed parcel.
06 Toast it pleats-down in a dry pan until golden, flip and toast the other side. Cut in half.

CHEF'S NOTE
Squeeze the potatoes as dry as you can: dry potatoes make the crisp pancake that holds the whole wrap together.

Kitchen test owed: the pancake's size against the small tortilla; the walnut mince's time.
