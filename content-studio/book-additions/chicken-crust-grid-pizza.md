# Chicken-Crust Grid Pizza (new recipe for the new PDF; day 1 of the healthy series, 14 Nov 2026)
Source: the research report `reports/وصفات صحية بطريقة الكرنكل.md`, card 1, written in our own words. Not in the book.
Owner approval (2026-11-13): «عادي اي وصفة مش مشكلة». UNTESTED: quantities and oven times are starting points; cook it
once before the PDF page is final. Macros are estimates from USDA values recalled from memory, not looked up.

Audience: gym women who lift 3-4 times a week and fall short of their protein target without shakes.
Serves 4, in one 20 x 30 cm black rectangular baking tray.

## Ingredients
Crust
- 500 g cooked chicken breast (poached and shredded fine, or halal canned chicken), squeezed dry
- 2 eggs (about 100 g)
- 40 g halal-certified parmesan-style hard cheese, finely grated
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano
Topping
- 150 g passata
- 120 g part-skim mozzarella, shredded
- 100 g red pepper, diced
- 100 g mushrooms, sliced
- 30 g baby spinach

## Method
1. Squeeze the shredded chicken hard in a clean towel until no more water comes out.
2. Mix the chicken with the eggs, cheese, garlic powder and oregano. Press it 1 cm thick into the parchment-lined tray.
3. Score a deep 4 x 6 grid into the crust with a bench scraper.
4. Bake the bare crust at 220°C for 20-25 minutes, until dry and golden. No sauce yet: this is the step that matters.
5. Spread the passata over the baked crust. Put one topping in each square, in a checkerboard, then the mozzarella.
6. Bake 8 minutes more, until the cheese bubbles. Cut along the scored lines.

## Per serving (estimate)
About 390 kcal, 54 g protein, 8 g carbs, 15 g fat, 1.7 g fibre.

## The mistake (the hook)
"Sauce on a raw crust is the mistake" / «الصلصة عالقاعدة النيّة هي الغلطة». Water on the surface holds the crust near
100°C, and browning needs about 140°C, so a sauced raw crust steams instead of crisping. Evidence: moderate (the sources
are about searing meat; applying it to a chicken crust is an inference).

## Halal
Halal-slaughtered chicken (canned included). Mozzarella and the parmesan-style cheese with microbial or halal-certified
rennet. No pork toppings (no pepperoni, salami or ham).
