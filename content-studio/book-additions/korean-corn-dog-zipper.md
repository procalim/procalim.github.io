## The addition: the zipper cut (before step 01; for the owner to approve and test)
00 Lay a hot dog flat. With the tip of a small knife, cut it lengthwise through its middle along a zigzag of small
   square teeth, about 5 mm each, leaving 2 cm uncut at one end. Pull the cut end apart into a V: the teeth show like
   an open zipper. Lay a mozzarella stick in the V and press the teeth back together, so the halves lock around the
   cheese. Then step 01: the skewer goes in through the uncut end.
Halal: halal beef hot dogs, halal-certified mozzarella.
