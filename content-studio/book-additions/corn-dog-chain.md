# Corn Dog Chain («سلسلة الكورن دوغ»): N° 059 Korean Corn Dog with an invented link (2026-11-20, approved by the owner with his two reference images; the kitchen test is still owed)
The owner, 20 Nov: «شو الفكرة الجاية؟ ... والفكرة معقدة نولد الها صورة لنقلل الأخطاء».
- **Why this idea:**
  - The lantern (v4) reached 25,300 accounts, 13x our median of 1,909.
  - The 58M AI reel is also a hot dog transformation.
  - The lantern cut, with a single slit, makes a loop. The next hot dog goes through it, and the links make a chain.
- **Has anyone made it?** A web search on 20 Nov found no corn dog or hot dog chain linked through slits. Sausages
  linked in their casing are a different thing.

## The book, word for word (N° 059, p206): as in korean-corn-dog-lantern.md

## The additions (for the owner to approve and test)
00 (our link) Cut one long slit through the middle of each hot dog (through both sides), leaving 1.5 cm whole at each
   end. Push the ends together and the slit opens into an oval loop. Pass the next hot dog, with a mozzarella stick
   beside it, through the loop, then open its own slit the same way. Four links make the chain. A skewer through the
   first link is the handle.
- **Steps 02-03:** the chain does not fit a tall glass, so the batter goes into a long deep tray. The chain is laid in
  it and turned until it is coated completely.
- **Freezing (step 01)** holds the links in shape. Frying (step 05) needs a wide pot.
- **Not tested yet:** the slit length, how the links hold together in the oil, and the cheese staying inside each loop.

## Halal
Halal-certified beef or chicken hot dogs, halal-certified mozzarella, vegetable oil for frying.
