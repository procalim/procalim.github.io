## Word for word
🍖 The Chef's Special: Golden-Seared Herb Short Ribs with Rich Demi-Glace
Fall-off-the-bone beef short ribs, seared to a deep golden crust and slow-braised in an aromatic herb reduction before
being lacquered in a glossy, velvety demi-glace.

🛒 Ingredients
1. The Short Ribs & Golden Sear
- 2.5 lbs (approx. 1.2 kg) Bone-in Beef Short Ribs (English-cut, thick and meaty)
- 1 tbsp Kosher salt & 1 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
- 2 tbsp Neutral high-heat oil (avocado or vegetable oil)
- 2 tbsp Unsalted butter
2. The Aromatic Braising Base
- 1 large Yellow onion (roughly chopped)
- 2 medium Carrots (cut into chunks)
- 2 Celery stalks (cut into chunks)
- 1 whole head of Garlic (sliced in half crosswise)
- 2 tbsp Tomato paste
- 1 cup Non-alcoholic red wine substitute (unsweetened pomegranate juice + 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar) OR dry red wine
- 2 cups Rich Beef Stock
3. The Herb Bundle & Golden Demi-Glace Finish
- 4 sprigs Fresh Rosemary
- 6 sprigs Fresh Thyme
- 3 sprigs Fresh Oregano (or Sage)
- 2 Bay leaves
- 1 cup Prepared Demi-Glace Sauce
- 1 tbsp Cold butter (cubed, for mounting the sauce)
- Fresh parsley (finely chopped, for garnish)

👩‍🍳 Cooking Instructions
Step 1: Achieve the Deep Golden Crust
1. Preheat your oven to 325°F (165°C).
2. Pat the beef short ribs completely dry with paper towels (moisture prevents browning). Season all sides aggressively with kosher salt and black pepper.
3. Heat the oil in a heavy Dutch oven or oven-safe pot over medium-high heat until shimmering.
4. Lay the short ribs in the pot in a single layer without crowding. Sear for 3–4 minutes per side until every edge develops a deep, golden-brown crust. Remove the ribs to a plate and set aside.
Step 2: Caramelize the Aromatics & Herbs
1. Pour off excess fat from the pot, leaving about 1 tablespoon behind. Lower heat to medium and toss in the chopped onion, carrots, celery, and the halved head of garlic (cut-side down).
2. Sauté for 5–6 minutes until the vegetables soften and turn golden.
3. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes until it darkens to a rich brick-red.
4. Pour in your liquid deglazer (pomegranate-balsamic mix or red wine) and scrape all the caramelized golden bits (the fond) off the bottom of the pot with a wooden spoon. Let it simmer and reduce by half.
Step 3: Slow-Braise with Fresh Herbs
1. Tie the rosemary, thyme, oregano, and bay leaves together with kitchen twine (or tuck them directly into the pot).
2. Return the golden-seared short ribs to the pot, nestled bone-side up into the vegetables.
3. Pour in the beef stock until the liquid comes about three-quarters of the way up the ribs (do not completely submerge the tops).
4. Bring to a gentle simmer on the stove, cover tightly with a lid, and transfer to the oven. Bake for 2.5 to 3 hours until the meat is fork-tender and pulling away from the bone.
Step 4: Build the Glossy Herb Demi-Glace
1. Carefully transfer the tender short ribs to a warm serving platter and tent loosely with foil.
2. Strain the remaining braising liquid through a fine-mesh sieve into a clean saucepan, pressing on the vegetables and herbs to extract every drop of flavor. Skim off any excess fat floating on top.
3. Whisk the demi-glace into the strained herb liquid and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Simmer for 8–10 minutes until reduced into a thick, glossy glaze that coats the back of a spoon.
4. Turn off the heat and whisk in the cold butter cubes one at a time until the sauce shines like liquid gold.
