# Cheesy Baked Eggplant Chicken Buns (new recipe for the new PDF; outside-account plan, 16 Nov 3 pm)
Source: fiteat.rima's reel DcUDu5xhdMi (3.5M plays), read frame by frame 2026-11-14, written in our own words. The
quantities are ours. UNTESTED: starting points. Macros are estimates. Makes 4 buns.

## Ingredients
- 2 large eggplants (8 rounds, 2 cm thick)
- 8 slices mozzarella (about 160 g, halal-certified)

The filling
- 300 g minced chicken breast (halal)
- 100 g spinach, chopped
- 100 g ricotta (no gelatin)
- 1 garlic clove, salt and pepper

The top
- 1 egg, beaten
- 30 g breadcrumbs mixed with 20 g grated hard cheese (halal-certified)

The tomatoes
- 200 g cherry tomatoes, 6 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp olive oil
- Chopped parsley to finish

## Method
1. Salt the eggplant rounds for 10 minutes and pat them dry.
2. Cook the minced chicken in a pan until no pink remains. Mix it with the spinach, ricotta, garlic, salt and pepper.
3. On 4 rounds lay a mozzarella slice, the filling, a second slice of mozzarella and a second round. Press gently.
4. Brush with the egg and cover with the crumb and cheese mix.
5. Put the tomatoes, garlic and oil in a ramekin next to the buns.
6. Bake at 200°C for 30-35 minutes, until the tops are deep golden and the eggplant is soft.
7. Top each bun with the roasted tomatoes and garlic, and finish with parsley.

## Per bun (estimate)
About 460 kcal, 42 g protein, 20 g carbs, 22 g fat.

## Halal
- Halal chicken.
- Mozzarella, hard cheese and ricotta halal-certified (no animal rennet unless halal, no gelatin).
