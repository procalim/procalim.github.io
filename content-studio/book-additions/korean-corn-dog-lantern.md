## The book, word for word (N° 059, p206)
Korean Corn Dog (Cheese Pull)
The famous street food with a crunchy sugar coating.

INGREDIENTS
— Hot dogs or Mozzarella sticks (cut into blocks)
— Skewers
— Batter: 1 cup Flour, 2 tbsp Sugar, ½ tsp Salt, 1 Egg
— ¾ cup Cold milk, 2 tsp Baking powder
— Panko breadcrumbs
— Sugar (for coating)

METHOD
01 Skewer the hot dogs and cheese. Freeze 20 mins (helps batter stick).
02 Whisk batter ingredients until thick and sticky. Pour into a tall glass.
03 Dip skewers into batter, coating completely.
04 Roll in Panko breadcrumbs (press them on).
05 Deep fry at 350°F (175°C) for 3–5 minutes until golden brown.
06 Roll in sugar immediately. Top with ketchup and mustard.

CHEF'S NOTE
Freezing before battering is the trick — the cold helps the batter stick evenly

## The addition: the lantern cut (before step 01; for the owner to approve and test)
00 Lay a hot dog flat. With the tip of a small knife, cut 6 slits lengthwise through the hot dog, all around it,
   leaving 1.5 cm uncut at each end. Push the two ends toward each other: the strips bow out into a lantern. Put a
   block of mozzarella inside the lantern. Then step 01: the skewer goes in through one end, through the cheese and out
   through the other end.
- **Why it should hold:** the step 01 freeze firms both the cage and the cheese. The thick batter in step 03 fills
  the gaps between the strips, so the cheese stays inside while it fries.
- **The payoff:** a bite or a cut shows a ring of hot dog strips around a core of melted cheese, and the cheese pulls
  out between the strips.
- **Not tested yet:** the slit count (6) and the 1.5 cm ends are a first guess for a standard hot dog.
