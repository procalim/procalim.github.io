Halal: chicken stock or halal beef stock (never pork bone), halal beef, a halal ramen packet.

Soft-yolk eggs, bok choy and braised beef over springy noodles in a hot, savoury broth.
Serves 1.

INGREDIENTS
— 1 packet instant ramen (halal), or 100 g dried ramen noodles
— 500 ml chicken stock (or halal beef stock)
— 1 tbsp soy sauce (halal-certified)
— 1 garlic clove, crushed
— 2 eggs
— 1 small bok choy, halved
— A small handful of enoki mushrooms
— 100 g halal braised beef (or seared beef steak), thinly sliced
— 1 green onion, sliced
— Chili oil, to finish (optional)

METHOD
01 Boil the eggs for 6½ minutes, then cool them in iced water and peel.
02 Bring the stock to a boil with the soy sauce and garlic.
03 Cook the noodles in the broth for 2-3 minutes, adding the bok choy and enoki for the last minute.
04 Pour the noodles, broth and greens into a deep bowl.
05 Halve the eggs and lay them on top with the beef slices and the green onion.
06 Finish with chili oil and eat hot.

CHEF'S NOTE
Six and a half minutes and straight into iced water: that is the soft, jammy yolk.
