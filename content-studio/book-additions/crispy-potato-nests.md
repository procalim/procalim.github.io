# Crispy Potato Nests (çıtır patates) - reference recipe from the owner (2026-11-12)
Source: a Turkish reel the owner sent as screenshots on 2026-11-12, its English caption copied word for word below.
Not in the book: it goes into the book (and a new PDF) only after the owner approves and tests it.

## Word for word (the reel's caption)
CRISPY POTATOES
Ingredients:
* 2 potatoes
* 4 tablespoons of flour
* 4 tablespoons of starch
* Half a bottle of mineral water
* half a teaspoon of turmeric
* Half a teaspoon of powdered red pepper.
* Just an enough salt
The making of:
Slice potatoes into thin pieces in a matchbox.
Take flour, starch, mineral water, turmeric, ground pepper and salt in a bowl and mix it well and prepare a ration.
Add the mashed potatoes to the casserole and mix them in a way that they will come all over the place.
Then fry the angry oil as shown in the video until it gets a golden yellow color.
You can serve it with ketchup-mayonnaise mix or with yogurt on the side. The choice is yours!

## What the machine translation means (from the Turkish), for the owner to confirm
- "in a matchbox": cut into matchsticks (Turkish kibrit çöpü, julienne).
- "a ration": a batter.
- "the mashed potatoes ... casserole": the cut potatoes go into the batter bowl and are mixed until coated all over.
- "the angry oil": hot oil (Turkish kızgın yağ). The video shallow-fries loose nests in a black pan.
- "mineral water": sparkling mineral water (Turkish maden suyu); its bottles are usually 200 ml, so half is about
  100 ml. To confirm.
- "starch": most likely corn starch. To confirm.
- Not stated: the oil and its amount (the video shows about 1 cm in a wide pan).

## Halal
Potatoes, flour, starch, water, spices, salt, frying oil; ketchup, mayonnaise and yogurt to serve. Nothing to change.
