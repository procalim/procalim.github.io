# Braided Blue Corn Dog («ضفيرة الكورن دوغ الزرقا»): N° 059 with an invented braid and a blue chip crust (2026-11-20)
The owner, 20 Nov: «بلش بالفكرة الثالث وبدل البانكو شيبس التاكو الأزرق وهشين الشيبس ما يطلع مخربط بالاسم بنولد الصور».
Ep 3 of «ما حدا عملها», after the lantern (v4: 25,300 reached) and the chain.

## Has anyone made it? (web search, 20 Nov)
- **The braid: not found.** No source has a hot dog cut into strands and braided. The closest are the spider cut, the
  octopus cut, the spiral cut, and a patent for battered segments.
- **The chip crust: made before, in yellow.** Corn dogs rolled in crushed tortilla chips already exist: a Tostitos
  recipe, and an andouille corn dog in yellow corn chips. Blue corn chips were not found. So the new parts are the
  braid and the blue.

## The book, word for word (N° 059, p206): as in korean-corn-dog-lantern.md

## The changes (for the owner to approve and test)
- **00 (our braid):**
  - A long halal beef hot dog is cut lengthwise into 2 strands, leaving 2 cm whole at the top.
  - A mozzarella string-cheese stick is peeled into a third strand and pressed to the top.
  - The three (pink, pink, white) are braided like hair and pinched at the bottom.
  - A skewer goes up through the braid.
- **Step 04: the owner's swap.** Crushed plain blue corn tortilla chips (salted only, no flavour dust) take the place of
  the panko.
- **Step 06 as written:** sugar, ketchup and mustard.
- **Not tested yet:** whether the thin strands hold in the batter and the oil, and whether the cheese strand melts
  without leaking before the pull.

## Halal
Halal-certified beef hot dogs, halal-certified mozzarella, plain blue corn chips (corn, oil, salt; no meat flavouring),
vegetable oil.
