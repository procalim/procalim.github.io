## N° 005 Marry Me Chicken (p152), word for word
Creamy Tuscan-style chicken said to be so good it elicits proposals.
INGREDIENTS
— 3 Chicken breasts (sliced lengthwise)
— ½ cup Flour (for dredging)
— 1 cup Heavy cream
— ½ cup Chicken broth
— ½ cup Sun-dried tomatoes (chopped)
— ¼ cup Parmesan cheese
— Garlic, Thyme, Red pepper flakes, Fresh basil
METHOD
01 Season chicken and dredge lightly in flour.
02 Sear chicken in a pan with oil/butter until golden. Remove and set aside.
03 Sauté garlic for 1 minute. Add chicken broth to deglaze pan.
04 Add heavy cream, sun-dried tomatoes, thyme, parmesan, and chili flakes. Simmer 5 minutes.
05 Return chicken to sauce. Simmer another 3–5 minutes.
06 Garnish with fresh basil. Serve over pasta or mashed potatoes.
CHEF'S NOTE
Don't skip dredging in flour — it gives the sauce a luxurious thickness

## Ours (for the owner's approval and a kitchen test)
In place of the pasta or mashed potatoes in step 06, it is served in a bread ring box, with an edible ring inside.
- **The box:** 1 round crusty bread roll (about 12 cm). Cut across its top third from the front, stopping 2 cm before
  the back, so the back crust stays as a hinge; lift the lid back and scoop out the soft crumb, leaving a 1 cm wall.
  Brush with butter and toast in a hot skillet or oven until crisp, 4-5 minutes.
- **The ring:** 2 tbsp grated parmesan (extra) scattered in a ring about 6 cm wide in a dry non-stick pan over medium
  heat, 2 minutes, until melted, lacy and golden; while still soft, press a piece of sun-dried tomato on top as the
  stone; it sets crisp in 1 minute.
- **Assembly:** basil leaves in the bottom of the box, a little grated parmesan, the sauce, the chicken; the ring stood
  upright in the centre; the lid closed. Opened at the table toward the person, like a ring box.
