# Spinach & Lentil Crinkle (new recipe for the new PDF; healthy series day 2)
Source: the research report `reports/وصفات صحية بطريقة الكرنكل.md`, card 2, written in our own words. Not in the book.
Owner approval (2026-11-13): «عادي اي وصفة مش مشكلة». UNTESTED: quantities and oven times are starting points. Macros are
estimates. Audience: young women told their iron is low, in homes where tea follows every meal. Serves 6.

## Ingredients
- 250 g phyllo sheets
- 500 g fresh spinach
- 100 g red lentils (dry)
- 150 g red pepper, diced
- 4 eggs (about 200 g)
- 250 g plain pasteurised Greek yogurt
- 30 g olive oil
- 30 ml lemon juice and the zest of 1 lemon, plus lemon halves to serve
- 1 tsp nigella seeds
- 1 tsp sumac
- A pinch of salt (about 1 g)

## Method
1. Cook the lentils for 10-12 minutes and drain.
2. Wilt the spinach, squeeze it dry and chop it. Mix with the lentils, red pepper, lemon zest and nigella seeds.
3. Brush each phyllo sheet lightly with oil, scrunch it into a ruffle and stand the ruffles upright, packed in rows in the tray.
4. Push the filling down into the folds. Bake at 180°C for 10 minutes, until the tips are crisp.
5. Whisk the eggs, yogurt and salt and pour over. Bake 30-35 minutes more, until fully set with no wobble.
6. Squeeze lemon over and sprinkle the sumac.
7. Serve with water. Have tea an hour later, not with the meal: that is the mistake step.

## Per serving (estimate)
About 328 kcal, 18 g protein, 39 g carbs, 11 g fat, 4.9 g fibre, about 5.4 mg iron (with iron-enriched phyllo; about
1.3 mg less without), about 58 mg vitamin C, about 400 mg sodium.

## The mistake (the hook)
"Tea after it is the mistake" / «الشاي بعد الأكل هو الغلطة». Tea polyphenols bind plant (non-haem) iron: black tea cut
iron absorption from a meal by 79-94% in isotope studies, tea 64% and coffee 39% from a meal; the vitamin C of lemon and
red pepper increases non-haem iron absorption. Strong for a single meal; smaller over a whole diet. The one-hour gap is a
practical rule, not a tested one. The lentils carry the iron; spinach iron is poorly absorbed.

## Safe wording
EN: "Lentils for iron, plus lemon and red pepper, whose vitamin C increases iron absorption. Have your tea an hour later, not with the meal."
AR: «عدس للحديد، مع ليمون وفلفل أحمر فيهم فيتامين C بيزيد امتصاص الحديد. اشربوا الشاي بعد ساعة، مش مع الأكل.»
Never: "cures anaemia", "raises haemoglobin", "replaces iron tablets". Add when it fits: if you think your iron is low,
get a blood test; diagnosed anaemia needs your doctor.

## Halal and safety
Read the phyllo label (flour, water, oil; any vinegar not wine vinegar). Yogurt with no gelatin. The custard fully set
before anyone pregnant eats it.
