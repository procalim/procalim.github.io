# Beef Tortilla Pinwheel Skewers with Green Yogurt Sauce (new recipe for the new PDF; outside-account plan, 18 Nov 3 pm)

Macros are estimates. Makes 8 skewers (2 rounds of 4 rolls).

## Ingredients
The rolls
- 8 large flour tortillas (about 25 cm)
- 800 g lean ground beef (halal)
- 1 red bell pepper, very finely chopped
- 1 small onion, grated and squeezed dry
- A handful of parsley, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp black pepper, 1½ tsp salt
- 200 g shredded cheddar and mozzarella (halal-certified)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 8 long wooden skewers

The green sauce
- 200 g Greek yogurt (no gelatin)
- A big handful each of coriander and parsley
- 1 garlic clove, juice of ½ lemon, ½ green chilli (optional), salt

## Method
1. Mix the beef with the pepper, onion, parsley, tomato paste, spices and salt.
2. Trim each tortilla into a square.
3. Spread a thin even layer of the beef to the edges, about 100 g a tortilla, and sprinkle with the cheese.
4. Roll each one up tightly.
5. Lay 4 rolls side by side and push 4 skewers through all 4, evenly spaced. Cut between the skewers. Repeat with the
   other 4 rolls.
6. Lay the skewers on a lined tray, brush with the oil and bake at 200°C for 20-25 minutes, until golden and the beef is
   cooked through.
7. Blend the sauce until smooth and drizzle it over the skewers.

## Per skewer (estimate)
About 520 kcal, 34 g protein, 38 g carbs, 25 g fat.

## Halal
- Halal beef.
- Cheese halal-certified.
- Greek yogurt with no gelatin.
