# N° 366 Accordion Sweet Potatoes «بطاطا حلوة أكورديون» (2026-11-27, a new recipe for the book)

N° 026 Accordion Potatoes (p173), for the owner's approval and a kitchen test. Halal: nothing to swap.

Sweet potato blocks sliced thin on a chopstick guide, brushed with spiced oil and roasted until the edges crisp.
Serves 2.

INGREDIENTS
— 2 large sweet potatoes
— 3 tbsp olive oil
— 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp dried herbs, salt and black pepper
— Chopped parsley, to finish
— 2 chopsticks

METHOD
01 Peel the sweet potatoes and trim each into a flat rectangular block about 2 cm thick.
02 Lay a block between two chopsticks and slice straight down every 2-3 mm; the chopsticks stop the knife before the bottom.
03 Mix the oil with the paprika, garlic powder, herbs, salt and pepper; brush it over the blocks and into the cuts.
04 Roast on a lined tray at 200°C for 30-35 minutes, until soft inside and crisp at the edges.
05 Finish with parsley.

CHEF'S NOTE
The chopsticks are the whole trick: every cut stops at the same depth, so the block fans open and stays in one piece.
