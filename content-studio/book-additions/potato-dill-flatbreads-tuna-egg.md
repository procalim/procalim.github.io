# Potato Dill Flatbreads with Tuna & Egg (new recipe for the new PDF; outside-account plan, 15 Nov 3 pm)
Source: fiteat.rima's reel Dcl_YHWtbkT (12.8M plays), read frame by frame 2026-11-13, written in our own words. The
quantities are ours. UNTESTED: starting points. Macros are estimates. Makes 4 cups.

## Ingredients
The cups
- 600 g potatoes, boiled and peeled
- 80 g plain flour
- 100 g shredded mozzarella (halal-certified)
- A small bunch of dill, chopped
- A pinch of salt

The filling
- 2 tins of tuna in water (about 320 g drained)
- 2 tbsp Greek yogurt (no gelatin)
- 1 tbsp tomato paste and 1 tsp harissa (check there is no wine)
- 1 tsp paprika and a squeeze of lemon

To finish
- 4 eggs, fried in 1 tbsp oil
- Basil leaves and lime halves

## Method
1. Mash the warm potatoes with the flour, mozzarella, dill and salt into a soft dough.
2. Roll 4 balls on a lined tray. Press the flat bottom of a glass into each one to make a cup with a raised rim.
3. Bake at 200°C for 25-30 minutes, until deep golden and crisp underneath.
4. Mix the tuna with the yogurt, tomato paste, harissa, paprika and lemon.
5. Fry the eggs until the whites are crisp and the yolks still runny.
6. Fill each cup with tuna, top with an egg and a basil leaf, and serve with lime.

## Per cup (estimate)
About 480 kcal, 37 g protein, 50 g carbs, 15 g fat.

## Halal and safety
- Tuna in water, mozzarella halal-certified, harissa with no wine.
- Runny yolks: for anyone pregnant, cook the yolk through.
