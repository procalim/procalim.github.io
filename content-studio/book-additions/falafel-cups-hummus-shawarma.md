# Baked Falafel Cups with Hummus & Chicken Shawarma (new recipe for the new PDF; outside-account plan, 15 Nov 10 pm)

quantities are ours. UNTESTED: starting points. Macros are estimates. Makes 4 cups.

## Ingredients
The cups
- 250 g dried chickpeas, soaked overnight (not tinned)
- A handful each of parsley and coriander
- ½ red onion and 2 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 40 g flour and ½ tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, salt
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds and 1 tbsp olive oil

The filling
- 200 g hummus
- 300 g halal chicken breast in strips, with 1 tsp shawarma spice, lemon, garlic and 1 tbsp oil
- 30 g toasted slivered almonds, chopped parsley
- 1 small red onion and 2 tomatoes, chopped, with sumac
- Tahini sauce: 2 tbsp tahini, lemon juice, water, salt

## Method
1. Blitz the drained chickpeas, herbs, onion and garlic into a coarse green mix.
2. Mix in the tahini, flour, baking powder, spices and salt.
3. Roll 4 balls on a lined tray and press the bottom of a glass into each to make a cup with a raised rim.
4. Sprinkle the sesame and drizzle the oil. Bake at 200°C for 25-30 minutes, until golden-brown and crisp.
5. Cook the chicken in a hot pan with the shawarma spice, lemon and garlic.
6. Fill each cup with hummus, then the chicken, almonds, parsley and salad. Drizzle the tahini sauce.

## Per cup (estimate)
About 650 kcal, 38 g protein, 60 g carbs, 32 g fat.

## Halal
- Halal chicken.
- Tahini and hummus with no added gelatin.
- No wine.
