## The book, word for word (N° 121, from src/data/recipes.ts, published verbatim from the book)
Korean Cream Cheese Garlic Bread
The viral Asian street food that is equal parts sweet, savory, and aggressively garlicky.

INGREDIENTS
— 4 Round bread rolls (brioche or soft dinner rolls)
— Cream cheese filling: 1 cup Cream cheese (softened), 2 tbsp Sugar, ¼ cup Heavy cream
— Garlic butter dip: ½ cup Butter (melted), ¼ cup Milk, 2 tbsp Honey, 3 tbsp Garlic (minced), 1 Egg, 2 tbsp Fresh parsley (chopped)

METHOD
01 Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
02 Cut each roll into 6 wedges, almost all the way down, leaving the base intact so it opens like a flower.
03 Whip the cream cheese, sugar, and heavy cream until smooth. Transfer to a piping bag.
04 Pipe the cream cheese generously between every single slice.
05 Whisk all the garlic butter dip ingredients together in a separate bowl.
06 Dunk each stuffed roll completely into the garlic butter, making sure it gets inside the cuts.
07 Bake for 15–20 minutes until the edges are crispy and the cream cheese is gooey.

CHEF'S NOTE
Leave the base uncut — it is the only thing holding the flower together

## The addition: the clam and the pearl (for the owner to approve and test)
00 Before step 02, cut the roll across through its middle, three quarters of the way, so the top opens on a hinge at
   the back like a shell. Step 02's six cuts go into the top shell only and fan out from the hinge, like the ribs of a
   scallop shell; the hinge and the base stay uncut.
04b After step 04, pipe a bed of cream cheese inside the shell, set one ball of mozzarella (about 4 cm) on it as the
   pearl, and close the shell. Then steps 05-07 as written.
Halal: brioche made without lard; halal-certified cream cheese and mozzarella.
