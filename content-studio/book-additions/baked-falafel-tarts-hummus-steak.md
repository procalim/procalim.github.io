# Baked Falafel Tarts with Hummus & Steak (new recipe for the new PDF; outside-account plan, 18 Nov 10 pm)

## Ingredients
The tarts
- 250 g dried chickpeas, soaked overnight (not tinned)
- A handful each of parsley and coriander
- ½ red onion and 2 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 40 g flour and ½ tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, salt
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds and 1 tbsp olive oil

The filling
- 2 sirloin steaks, about 400 g (halal)
- 1 tsp seven spices, 1 garlic clove, salt, pepper, 1 tbsp olive oil
- 200 g hummus
- 30 g toasted slivered almonds, chopped parsley
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced, with the juice of 1 lemon and a pinch of salt and sugar
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- Tahini sauce: 2 tbsp tahini, lemon juice, water, salt

## Method
1. Leave the sliced onion in the lemon juice, salt and sugar for 15 minutes, until pink.
2. Blitz the drained chickpeas, herbs, onion and garlic into a coarse green mix.
3. Mix in the tahini, flour, baking powder, spices and salt.
4. Roll 4 balls on a lined tray and press the base of a glass into each to make a wide tart with a raised rim.
5. Sprinkle the sesame and drizzle the oil. Bake at 200°C for 25-30 minutes, until golden-brown and crisp.
6. Season the steaks with the spices, garlic, salt and pepper, sear in the hot oil for 3-4 minutes a side, rest for
   5 minutes and slice thin.
7. Fill each tart with hummus, then the steak, almonds and parsley, the pink onion and tomato. Drizzle the tahini sauce.

## Per tart (estimate)
About 730 kcal, 44 g protein, 62 g carbs, 34 g fat.

## Halal
- Halal beef.
- Tahini and hummus with no added gelatin.
- No wine.
