## N° 106 The Onion Boil (p255), word for word
The viral seafood boil experience, but made with a whole, juicy onion.
INGREDIENTS
— 1 Large Yellow or White Onion (peeled, top and bottom trimmed flat)
— 1 stick Butter (melted)
— 2 cloves Garlic (minced)
— 1 tbsp Old Bay Seasoning
— 1 tsp Paprika, 1 tsp Lemon pepper, 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
— Squeeze of Lemon juice
METHOD
01 Cut a deep "X" into the top of the onion, cutting about 3/4 of the way down (do not cut all the way through, so the
   base stays intact).
02 Place the onion on a large square of heavy-duty aluminum foil.
03 Mix the melted butter, garlic, lemon juice, and all the spices in a bowl.
04 Pour the seasoned butter directly over and inside the cuts of the onion.
05 Wrap the foil tightly around the onion to completely seal it.
06 Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 45–50 minutes until the onion is incredibly soft and practically melting into the spicy
   butter sauce.

## N° 143 Volcano Cheese Ramen (p294), word for word
A towering, erupting bowl of spicy noodles smothered in a molten cheese lava.
INGREDIENTS
— 1 packet Spicy Korean Ramen (like Shin Ramyun)
— 1 Egg
— 1/2 cup Mozzarella cheese
— 1 slice American cheese
— 1 tsp Gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
— Green onions
METHOD
01 Boil the ramen noodles in a small pot with the soup base until just cooked. Do not drain the broth.
02 Pile the noodles high in the center of the pot to look like a mountain peaking out of the broth.
03 Crack the raw egg directly into the center of the noodle mountain.
04 Cover the noodles and egg entirely with the mozzarella and American cheese.
05 Cover the pot with a lid for 2 minutes until the cheese is completely melted and oozing down the sides like lava.
   Top with chili flakes and green onions.

## Ours (for the owner's approval and a kitchen test)
After N° 106's bake, open the foil and pull the soft centre layers out with tongs, leaving a crater. N° 143 is then
built in the onion instead of the pot: the noodles (step 01) piled into the crater as a mountain (02), the egg (03),
the two cheeses (04); the foil closed over the top in place of the lid (05), back in the oven for 5 minutes so the egg
sets and the cheese melts down the petals like lava. Chili flakes and green onions on top.
Kitchen test: does the cheese run down the petals, and does the egg set in 5 minutes?
