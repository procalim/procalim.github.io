# Sweet Potato Steak Bites (new recipe for the new PDF; outside-account plan, reel 1)

## Ingredients
The cups
- 1 large sweet potato (about 300 g flesh once baked)
- 120 g plain flour (about 1 cup), plus a little for the hands
- 100 g shredded mozzarella (halal-certified, microbial rennet)
- 15 g butter
- A pinch of salt

The steak
- 300 g halal beef steak (sirloin or ribeye), in 2 cm cubes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

The top
- 150 g cherry tomatoes
- 1 small red onion, sliced
- 1 avocado, sliced
- A few sprigs of fresh dill and parsley

The green sauce
- 200 g plain Greek yogurt (no gelatin)
- A handful of fresh coriander and parsley
- 1 small garlic clove
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- A pinch of salt

## Method
1. Prick the sweet potato and bake at 200°C for 45-50 minutes until soft. Let it cool enough to handle, then peel it.
2. Mash the flesh with the flour, mozzarella, butter and salt, and knead into a soft orange dough.
3. Roll 4 balls and set them on a lined tray. Press the flat bottom of a glass into each one to make a cup with a raised rim.
4. Bake at 200°C for 20-25 minutes, until the underside is deep golden and crisp.
5. While they bake:
   - Blister the cherry tomatoes and soften the onion in a hot pan.
   - Blend the green sauce ingredients until smooth.
6. Sear the steak cubes in the oil in a very hot pan, 1-2 minutes a side, then season.
7. Fill each cup with the steak, then the tomatoes, onion and herbs, then the avocado. Pour the green sauce over.

## Per cup (estimate)
About 540 kcal, 32 g protein, 47 g carbs, 25 g fat.

## Halal check
- Beef halal.
- Mozzarella halal-certified (no animal rennet unless halal).
- Yogurt with no gelatin.
- No wine, no alcohol, no pork.
- Pressed with the bottom of a plain tumbler. Her reel uses the foot of a stemmed glass, so ours never shows a wine glass.
