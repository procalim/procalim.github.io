Halal: halal beef mince; cheese halal-certified.

Roasted sweet potato halves mashed in their skins, loaded with cheese, spiced beef, pico de gallo and avocado.
Serves 1.

INGREDIENTS
The sweet potato
— 1 large sweet potato
— Cooking oil spray
— Salt and black pepper
The topping
— 50 g shredded mozzarella (halal-certified)
— 150 g halal beef mince
— 1 tsp taco seasoning (or ½ tsp each cumin, paprika and garlic powder, and salt)
— 3 tbsp pico de gallo (1 tomato, ¼ red onion, ¼ green pepper, coriander and lime juice, all finely diced)
— ½ avocado, thinly sliced
— A pinch of salt

METHOD
01 Cut the sweet potato in half lengthwise. Spray the cut sides with oil and season with salt and pepper.
02 Lay the halves cut-side down on a parchment-lined tray and roast at 200°C for 25-30 minutes, until soft and
   caramelized underneath.
03 Brown the beef mince in a dry pan, breaking it up, then stir in the seasoning and cook 2 minutes.
04 Put the halves cut-side up in a bowl and mash the flesh inside the skins with a fork.
05 Top in this order: the cheese, the beef, the pico de gallo, then fan the avocado over the centre and salt it.

CHEF'S NOTE
Roast the halves cut-side down: the cut face caramelizes against the tray and the inside steams soft for mashing.
