Crinkle Cake Recipe: the butter mistake almost everyone makes
Most people brush butter between every phyllo layer. That is the step that makes crinkle cake heavy and soft.

Mine goes into the oven completely dry for the first 10 minutes. The sheets set into crisp ridges, and only then does the butter go on, poured over the top in one go.
