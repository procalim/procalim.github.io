Food psychology: the judges decided before the first bite
3 senses judge your food before your tongue does.

Your eyes: plating changes taste. In an Oxford study, the same salad arranged like a painting was rated tastier, and people would pay more for it. Your nose: much of what we call taste is really smell. Your ears: a louder crunch made identical chips feel crisper and fresher.
