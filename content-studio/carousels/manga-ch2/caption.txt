Food psychology: why is there always room for dessert?
You were full. Then dessert landed.

It isn't weak will. In 1981 an Oxford team fed people one food until they were full. Their pleasure in it dropped more than for the foods they had not eaten. You get full of one food, not of food. A later test went further: four different courses made people eat about 60% more than the same dish four times. And in 2025 a German study in mice found that the very neurons that signal "full" also open a door for sugar.
