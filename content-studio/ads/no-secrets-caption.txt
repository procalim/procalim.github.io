Crinkle cake is scrunched phyllo baked until crisp, soaked in butter, set with a milk and egg custard, then finished with cold syrup poured on the hot cake. That temperature gap is what keeps the top crisp. This crinkle cake is recipe 113 in my book, and I'm not hiding it. Comment CRINKLE and I'll send you the link to the full recipe in my book.
