## Every reel
1. **The evening before:** I pull her reel, measure it, upload it to Higgsfield as @Video1, and write the prompt with halal food. The recipe goes to book-additions (the new PDF).
2. **The owner generates it:** Seedance 2.5 Omni reference, 720p, Chef-Ahmad element, the kitchen image.
3. **I edit it to her own shot timeline:**
   - her frame 0;
   - her text if she has one;
   - our small title;
   - the keyword over the payoff;
   - pp.py DEAD 0 and QUIET 0.
4. **The package:**
   - caption, English then Arabic;
   - pinned comment;
   - the DM pair: this recipe free, then the book link.
5. **Read at 24 h** against the sweet potato (23K at 21.5 h, 8.7 shares per 1K reach). The family that wins moves up the list (her method: repeat the winner).
6. **Backups if a take fails:** short ribs, chicken-crust pizza, spinach crinkle (edited, not posted).
