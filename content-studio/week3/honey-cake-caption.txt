Honey Cake Recipe that looks like fettuccine Alfredo 👀
Send this to the friend who'd swear it's pasta.

Honey cake fettuccine is a thin honey sponge, cut warm into long ribbons and tossed in a honey cream sauce, then finished with white chocolate grated like parmesan. It looks like fettuccine Alfredo and tastes like honey cake. Cut the sponge while it's warm: it bends like pasta. Cold, it cracks.
