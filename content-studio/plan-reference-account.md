## Limits (to weigh, not to block)
- Not guaranteed: her 11.2M reel became 23K on this account; her reach comes from her audience and history.
- Instagram has favoured original content since 2024 and cuts the reach of content that copies another account's.
- Her videos and photos are her work: a frame-for-frame copy can be reported, and repeated copyright reports can take
  videos down and restrict an account that sells a book. Recipe ideas and ingredient lists are free to use; her
  wording, photos, covers and videos are not.
- Her recipes may not be halal; every recipe is checked (halal only) and written in our own words, tested
  quantities, our own photos.
