# Three viral home-trick reels against ours (27 Nov)
The owner: «سحبت 3 فيديوهات ... احنا عم نعمل هيك ... بس الفيديوهات اللي عنا ... مش عم تنجح ... اعطيني سبب واحد».
His files: Cherie (accordion sweet potatoes, 11.9 s), Ayşe Nur Kocaoğlu (strawberry flower pancakes, 11.4 s), Nurgül Kotan
(cheesy smashed potatoes, 10.6 s); the owner puts the two potato reels at about 310M views each.

| Accordion sweet potatoes | the finished dish with its name on it, then a hand pulls a slice apart (0.9 s) | peel, square off, two chopsticks either side so the knife stops, slice; brush; oven | the tray, the plate, a knife through one |

| Smashed potatoes | oil and cheese on paper, a boiled potato on top | a wine glass smashes it flat (1.3-3.2 s), spice, oil, cheese, oven | knife through the crust, dipped in the white sauce |

What all three share: one cheap everyday ingredient, one trick with a household tool (chopsticks, a squeeze bottle, a
glass), real hands in daylight on a home counter, the food filling the frame from frame one, no face, no story, no
title cards beyond the dish's name, 10-12 s. The viewer's thought is «I can do this tonight», so they save it and send it.

Ours: a chef in a dark cinematic kitchen, restaurant dishes (a ring-pressed salad tower, a demi-glace braise, a fire
ramen, a crunchwrap), story hooks on top. The thought is «nice show». The account's own numbers agree (90 days): the
home-practical reels saved 11-17 a 1,000 reach (the stuffed sweet potato patties, the flatbreads, the macaron), the
show reels 0-2; the Salad Tower took 26,698 views and 1.6 saves a 1,000; the short ribs 0-1 saves.

The one reason: our reels are a show to watch; theirs are a trick anyone can do tonight.
Both potato tricks are in the owner's book: N° 015 Smashed Potatoes (p162) and N° 026 Accordion Potatoes (p173).
