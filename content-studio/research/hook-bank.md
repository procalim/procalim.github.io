### Cross-platform literature (directional, vendor and blog data, no platform study)
- The keep-or-skip decision lands at about 1.7 s (TikTok), 2.1 s (Reels), 2.5 s (Shorts): the hook's first line must
  be readable by about 1.5 s ([eliro.pro](https://eliro.pro/blog/short-form-video-hooks-script-guide-2026),
  [bytecap](https://www.bytecap.io/research/best-hooks-for-youtube-shorts)).
- Most viewers start muted, so the hook belongs on screen as text
  ([bytecap, silent viewing](https://www.bytecap.io/blog/science-of-silent-viewing-short-form-video-retention)).
- Curiosity gap and contradiction ("you're doing it wrong") are the most repeated formulas; no platform data ranks
  them ([TrueFuture Media](https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/science-of-short-form-video-hooks),
  [clipspeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/video-hook-ideas)).
- Title cards and logos in 0-3 s cost retention (agency data, 15-30%)
  ([perceedigital](https://www.perceedigital.com/insights/video-hooks-that-convert/)).

## M. The POV / meme line (owner, 2026-11-20: «نجمع بنك هوكات كتابة pov مضحكة زي الألماني»; untested on our account)
- **Source:** the format of @veganer.wandel (research/veganer-wandel-format.md). His outliers (571x, 25x, 16x, 12x) open
  on a meme line in a white box at the top: "when Germans get hungry at 3 a.m.", "how Germans get their protein",
  "Germans at 3:59 pm / 4:00 pm". That is an identity plus a moment everyone knows, over the first 3-4 s. His flop had
  no line and no skit.
- **Rules:**
  - 8 words at most, one line, in a white box at the top, 0-3.5 s only. The video is clean after it (the owner's no-text
    rule stays for the rest).
  - The place: 46 px, centred on y 290 of 1080x1920, above the food and the hands. Both chains put it in the middle
    (y 960), over the hand and the chain: skip 32.4% (20 s) and 34.3% (14 s) against 22.9% for the lantern with no
    text. Owner, 21 Nov: «نفس مكان pov خاطء».
  - The line names a moment everyone has lived; the picture answers it.
  - Never the recipe's name, and never the answer.
  - English for reels that travel (the lantern v4: 100% non-followers; Thailand, Indonesia, the US, Brazil, Mexico).
    Arabic for posts to the followers.
  - Self-irony only. No group made the butt of the joke, and nothing about religion.
- **Test:**
  - one reel with the line against one without, the same video, both as Trial Reels at the same hour;
  - read skip, the 3 s point, sends per 1K and keyword comments;
  - formula letter M in timeline.json; hook_check scores M 1/2 until it has its own numbers.
