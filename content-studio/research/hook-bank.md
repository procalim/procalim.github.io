### Cross-platform literature (directional, vendor and blog data, no platform study)
- The keep-or-skip decision lands at about 1.7 s (TikTok), 2.1 s (Reels), 2.5 s (Shorts): the hook's first line must
  be readable by about 1.5 s ([eliro.pro](https://eliro.pro/blog/short-form-video-hooks-script-guide-2026),
  [bytecap](https://www.bytecap.io/research/best-hooks-for-youtube-shorts)).
- Most viewers start muted, so the hook belongs on screen as text
  ([bytecap, silent viewing](https://www.bytecap.io/blog/science-of-silent-viewing-short-form-video-retention)).
- Curiosity gap and contradiction ("you're doing it wrong") are the most repeated formulas; no platform data ranks
  them ([TrueFuture Media](https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/science-of-short-form-video-hooks),
  [clipspeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/video-hook-ideas)).
- Title cards and logos in 0-3 s cost retention (agency data, 15-30%)
  ([perceedigital](https://www.perceedigital.com/insights/video-hooks-that-convert/)).
