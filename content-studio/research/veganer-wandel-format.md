used (Instagram terms). Last 60 shorts: the median is 28,000 views. Five videos were watched frame by frame on the

## The structure every outlier shares (4 of 4 watched; the flop had none of it)
1. **0-4 s, an identity skit in a bright real-life place, not the kitchen:**
   - one long shot with his face, physical comedy, and the hero ingredient used as a prop (thrown, caught, held up
     like a trophy, pulled out of a backpack);
   - a meme line in a white box at the top: "when Germans get hungry at 3 a.m.", "how Germans get their protein",
     "Germans at 3:59 pm / 4:00 pm" (two people drop their tools at 4 pm and grab a net of potatoes), "Germans
     checking the Kühlschrank on Sunday" (a fridge POV, a leek thrown in the air);
   - the identity (the nationality) plus a moment everyone knows.
2. **A hard cut into the dark cinematic kitchen:**
   - warm side light, steam, macro, true top-downs, his tattooed hands, his face at work in some shots;
   - 0.5-0.8 cuts per second (shots about 1.3-2 s).
3. **The end goes back to the skit's place** (eating it on the sofa, back at the wall): the story closes, then loops.
4. **Sound:** a music bed (the auto-captions read "[Musik]") with the food sounds, and one short spoken word at the
   end ("Kartoffel… beste").
5. **Length:** 27-33 s.
The flop (0.1x) opened straight on food in coloured light, with fast cuts (1.08 cuts per second), and no skit. Our
kibbeh flop matches it (fast cuts, hands only, 0.3x).

## What we take (format only; our data still applies)
- The identity-skit hook for the first 3-4 s, with his face, the hero ingredient as a prop, and an Arab identity
  line: Arab moments everyone knows. Mansaf (identity) was our 14.2x; the shawarma controversy 3.3x.
- The montage pace (0.5-0.8 cuts per second), the dark cinematic kitchen, the macro and steam: we already do this.
- The bookend ending back at the skit place.
- Unchanged from our data: a statement hook, the loudest money shot, his identity look, 20 s (owner), halal, the book
  or our own recipes.
- To test: a light music bed under the food sounds (Kevin uses music; our rule was no music). One variable at a
  time.
