## 1. Disease prevalence: diabetes/prediabetes, obesity, PCOS, cholesterol, hypertension, anaemia, IBS, gestational diabetes

### Takeaway
On absolute numbers, the region is one of the worst in the world for diabetes. Age-standardised adult diabetes is about 21-26% in the Gulf and Egypt (IDF Atlas, 11th edition, 2024 estimates), and roughly 62-64% of cases are undiagnosed. Adult female obesity is close to or above half in KSA, Kuwait, Iraq and Egypt. For women aged 18-34, though, the conditions that are both common and personally felt are PCOS (about 16-23% in Saudi/GCC general-population studies, about 30% among infertile women), anaemia (about 28-44% of women of reproductive age), weight, IBS/bloating (often above 20% in Saudi female student samples) and pregnancy-related GDM (about 13-15%). Diabetes itself skews older.

### Cited Findings

**Type 2 diabetes and prediabetes (IDF Diabetes Atlas 11th ed., 2025 release, 2024 estimates, adults 20-79)**
- Global: 589 million adults with diabetes in 2024 (11.1% of adults). The 11th edition was published in Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology (epub Dec 2025) — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41412135/)
- Global prediabetes: 635 million adults (1 in 8) have impaired glucose tolerance (IGT) and 488 million have impaired fasting glucose (IFG) — [IDF 11th ed. Global Factsheet](https://media.idf.org/media/uploads/sites/3/2025/04/IDF_Atlas_11th_Edition_2025_Global-Factsheet.pdf)
- MENA region: 85 million adults with diabetes and 50 million with IGT in 2024 — [IDF MENA page](https://idf.org/our-network/regions-and-members/middle-east-and-north-africa/). The Atlas regional page gives 17.6% prevalence and projects 163 million by 2050 — [IDF Atlas MENA](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-location/region/middle-east-and-north-africa/). A 2026 review citing the Atlas gives a MENA age-standardised prevalence of 19.9% in 2024, rising to 22.8% by 2050 — [Karamzad et al., Health Sci Rep 2026](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/hsr2.72932). The regional figures (17.6% vs 19.9%) disagree, probably because one is crude and one is age-standardised.
- **Saudi Arabia:** 23.1% of adults, 5,344,600 adults with diabetes, out of an adult population of 24,983,100 (2024) — [IDF Saudi Arabia member page](https://idf.org/our-network/regions-and-members/middle-east-and-north-africa/members/saudi-arabia/). The Atlas age-standardised row reads "19.6 · 23.1 · 25.4", which implies about 25.4% by 2050 — [IDF Atlas age-adjusted prevalence](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-indicator/diabetes-estimates-20-79-y/age-adjusted-comparative-prevalence-of-diabetes/). KSA is in the global top ten for diabetes prevalence — [IDF Atlas KSA page](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-location/country/saudi-arabia/)
- **Egypt:** 22.4% age-standardised, 13,214,500 adults with diabetes, out of an adult population of 66,602,500 (2024). Projected 24.7 million by 2050. About 62% are undiagnosed, roughly 8.2 million people — [IDF Egypt member page](https://idf.org/our-network/regions-and-members/middle-east-and-north-africa/members/egypt/); [IDF Atlas Egypt](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-location/country/egypt/)
- **UAE:** 20.7% age-standardised (2024), projected 22.7% by 2050; 64% undiagnosed — [IDF Atlas UAE](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-location/country/united-arab-emirates/)
- **Kuwait:** 25.6% age-standardised (2024 column inferred), projected 28.2% by 2050 — [IDF Atlas age-adjusted prevalence table](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-indicator/diabetes-estimates-20-79-y/age-adjusted-comparative-prevalence-of-diabetes/). Older national survey (2011-2014): 19.1% adjusted, with men 22.4% and women 14.4% — [Kuwait Diabetes Epidemiology Program, PMC7694112](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7694112/)
- **Qatar:** 24.6% age-standardised (2024 column inferred), projected 27.0% by 2050 — [IDF Atlas age-adjusted prevalence table](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-indicator/diabetes-estimates-20-79-y/age-adjusted-comparative-prevalence-of-diabetes/)
- **Iraq:** 13.4% age-standardised (2024 column inferred), projected 14.9% by 2050 — [IDF Atlas age-adjusted prevalence table](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-indicator/diabetes-estimates-20-79-y/age-adjusted-comparative-prevalence-of-diabetes/)
- **Jordan:** about 1.1 million adults with diabetes in 2024, projected 2.2 million by 2050. No prevalence % was retrievable — [IDF Atlas Jordan](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-location/country/jordan/). Jordan age-standardised IGT (prediabetes) row: "8.3 · 9.4 · 10.2", which implies about 9.4% in 2024 (column inferred) — [IDF Atlas IGT indicator](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-indicator/impaired-glucose-tolerance-igt-estimates-20-79-y/age-standardised-prevalence-of-igt/)
- Older counts (2021): Egypt about 6.09 million and KSA about 3.29 million adults with diabetes in a GBD-based MENA analysis. These are not comparable with the IDF 2024 numbers — [Karamzad et al. 2026, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13438175/)

**Obesity / overweight (women)**
- Adult female obesity, from World Obesity's ranking of NCD-RisC-based estimates labelled 2024: Egypt about 59% (9th globally), Iraq about 49.0%, Saudi Arabia about 48.5%, Kuwait about 48.4% — [World Obesity ranking PDF](https://data.worldobesity.org/tables/ranking-obesity-by-country-adults-1.pdf). Caveat: these values come from a search-result excerpt and could not be checked against the NCD-RisC Lancet 2024 appendix ([NCD-RisC Lancet 2024](https://www.thelancet.com/pdfs/journals/lancet/PIIS0140-6736(23)02750-2.pdf)). The Egypt figure in particular should be verified.
- Jordan: 43.1% obesity among women of reproductive age (JPFHS 2017-18) — [PMC11783968](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11783968/)
- GBD 2021 (adults 25+, overweight plus obesity combined): Egypt women 87% (2021) — [Lancet GBD 2021 forecasting study](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736%2825%2900355-1/fulltext)

**PCOS**
- GCC infertile women: pooled prevalence 30.0% from 7 studies (4 from KSA), I² about 92%. Saudi studies range from 18% to 56% (2014-2022). No UAE or Bahrain studies of infertile women were found — [Heliyon 2024 meta-analysis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2405844024166340)
- GCC general-population studies report a range from 4.6% (Oman) to 27.6% (UAE), with KSA and Kuwait at about 16% — [GBD PCOS burden in GCC 1990-2023, PMC13660512](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13660512)
- A pooled analysis of 24 studies (77,890 women) gives 17.59% overall, ranging from 6.56% in Oman to 23.0% in Saudi Arabia. The publication-date metadata looks inconsistent, so verify — [IJERPH 2026, doi 10.3390/ijerph23081085](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph23081085)
- Older Middle East pooled estimate: 8.9% (NIH criteria) and 11.9% (Rotterdam criteria) — cited in [Heliyon 2024](https://www.cell.com/heliyon/fulltext/S2405-8440(24)16634-0)

**Anaemia in women of reproductive age (15-49)**
- Jordan: 32% of ever-married women aged 15-49 are anaemic (Jordan Population and Family Health Survey 2023); prevalence has been rising over the past decade — [PMC12023650](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12023650/)
- EMR review covering 2000-2019 (so these are most likely 2019 WHO modelled values): Saudi Arabia 43.7%, Jordan 34.7%, Iraq 29.1%, Egypt 28.5% — [Archives of Medical Science / PMC12887953](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12887953/)
- FAO: Iraq, Oman and Egypt made the most progress on women's anaemia, while Jordan worsened — [FAO SOFI Near East 2023](https://openknowledge.fao.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/d18451b6-1c80-40be-b30b-dea116c532d7/content/sofi-statistics-rne-2023/aneamia-among-women.html)
- WHO 2025 global anaemia key findings: most countries in all regions show no progress or are getting worse — [WHO global anaemia estimates 2025](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/ffbc07c6-420c-4711-8589-2a7b6f3a3da2/content). Country-level 2023 values are on the [WHO GHO indicator](https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/prevalence-of-anaemia-in-women-of-reproductive-age-(-)) but could not be extracted.

**Hypertension and high cholesterol**
- Saudi Arabia (2019 KSA World Health Survey, analysed 2023): hypertension 9.2% in adults 15+. A later analysis of the same survey gives a crude 12.69%, with only 34.8% of hypertensive adults aware of it — [PMC12930371](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12930371/); [ResearchGate 2023 national analysis](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/370160044_National_and_regional_prevalence_rates_of_hypertension_in_Saudi_Arabia_A_descriptive_analysis_using_the_national_survey_data)
- Saudi high-cholesterol studies range from 8.5% to 54.9% depending on region and clinic population (Al-Kharj study, 2016 data) — [PMC7792200](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7792200/)
- Egypt (STEPS 2011-12, old): about 40% raised blood pressure and 37% raised cholesterol among adults 15-65 — [WHO EMRO Egypt STEPS release](https://www.emro.who.int/egy/egypt-events/ncd-launch-dec-12.html)

**IBS / bloating**
- Saudi systematic review: Rome IV prevalence 20.17% in women in pooled analysis; reported range 2.7%-83.3% across 22 studies — [Cureus systematic review](https://www.cureus.com/articles/341076-epidemiology-and-risk-factors-of-irritable-bowel-syndrome-in-the-saudi-population-a-systematic-review.pdf); [PMC11966369](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11966369/)
- Saudi multi-university study: 31.9% of 379 students met Rome IV for IBS — [PMC11081599](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11081599/)

**Gestational diabetes (GDM)**
- MENA 2000-2019 meta-analysis (16 countries): pooled GDM prevalence 13.0% (95% CI 11.5-14.6). GCC subregion 14.7% (highest), North Africa about 13.5% — [Al-Rifai et al., Front Endocrinol 2021, PMC8427302](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8427302/)
- An Eastern Mediterranean meta-analysis of 38 studies gives pooled GDM of 11% (95% CI 9-14%) — [BMC Endocr Disord 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12902-025-01959-4)

### Inferences
- Gulf adults outside the 18-34 range carry the largest diabetes headcount: Egypt 13.2M and KSA 5.3M in 2024. That makes "blood-sugar-friendly" and "diabetic-friendly" recipes highly shareable, because daughters forward them to parents. Within the brand's own 18-34 female audience, prediabetes/insulin resistance, PCOS and GDM are the personal entry points.
- The 62-64% undiagnosed rate (Egypt, UAE) supports a "you might not know your blood sugar is high" hook over a "for diabetics" label.
- Anaemia hits roughly 1 in 3 young women in Jordan, Iraq and Egypt, and up to 44% in KSA (2019), but it has a weak online identity compared with PCOS (see Q3). It is better used as a secondary angle (iron-rich, halal red meat/lentils) than as a lead audience.
- Hypertension and cholesterol prevalence is high in Egypt (about 40% / 37%, 2011-12), but both conditions are older-skewing and have low awareness (34.8% aware in KSA). They are weaker leads for women 18-34.

### Gaps
- No full IDF 2024 country table could be read (network block). Column years for Kuwait, Qatar, Iraq and Jordan IGT were inferred from row structure, using the UAE row (20.7 = 2024) as the anchor.
- No IDF 2024 prevalence % for Jordan, and no 2024 IGT/IFG figures for the other countries.
- No 2023 WHO country values for anaemia (the GHO chart was not extractable).
- No 2023-2026 national hypertension or cholesterol figures for Egypt, Jordan or Iraq. Egypt STEPS 2017 figures were not retrieved.
- No national PCOS, IBS or GDM figures for Egypt, Jordan or Iraq.
- The NCD-RisC 2022 female obesity values could not be confirmed directly. The Egypt about 59% value should be checked.

## 2. Gym and fitness culture: membership growth, Vision 2030 targets, women's participation since 2017-18, protein demand

### Takeaway
Saudi adult physical activity has risen from about 13% weekly participation before Vision 2030 (2015) to about 59-60% of adults meeting 150 min/week (GASTAT 2024-2025). Women lag men (43-45% vs 63-67%), but women are the fastest-growing gym segment (about 13% CAGR forecast). Women-only gym licences began in 2017-2018.

### Cited Findings
- GASTAT 2024 Physical Activity bulletin (Dec 2024): 58.5% of Saudi adults (18+) do at least 150 min of physical activity per week — [SPA](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2216764); [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/647522); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-585-of-adults-active-but-youth-sedentary-habits-on-the-rise-1.1733140777697). The same bulletin reports 23.2% of males vs 14% of females "practising" physical activity, on a different and stricter definition — [KSA.com summary](https://www.ksa.com/general/gastat-physical-activity-statistics-saudi-arabia-2024/)
- GASTAT 2025 Physical Activity statistics: participation 66.5% for males vs 43.1% for females — [SPA 2025 release](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2426843). The latest headline is 59.9% of adults meeting 150 min/week — [SPA on X](https://x.com/Spa_Eng/status/2113225675423289584); [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/665636/saudi-arabia/nearly-60-of-saudi-adults-meet-weekly-physical-activity-threshold-gastat-reports); earlier release 59.1% — [GASTAT](https://www.stats.gov.sa/en/w/news/108)
- GASTAT Saudi Women's Statistics Report 2024: 44.6% of Saudi women 18+ meet the 150 min/week threshold — [GASTAT women's report PDF](https://www.stats.gov.sa/documents/20117/2435273/Saudi+Women+Statistics+2024_EN+%281%29.pdf/9aa5a79c-cea5-9239-bd9f-ece710b6c33d); [SPA](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2382642)
- Vision 2030 weekly sports participation: about 13% before Vision 2030, 24% by 2020 (per a policymaker quote). The paper also notes women's activity rose after the Vision launched — [Physical activity policy in Saudi Arabia, Int J Sport Policy 2023](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/19406940.2023.2228812). The often-quoted 2030 target is 40% weekly participation (from search summaries; not confirmed in a primary Vision 2030 document).
- Women's gym licences: announced Feb 2017 — [The New Arab 2017](https://www.newarab.com/society/2017/2/14/Saudi-women-can-finally-hit-the-gym); [ExpatWoman](https://www.expatwoman.com/saudi-arabia/health-fitness/saudi-arabia-will-start-granting-licences-for-womens-gyms). The first female-only gym licences were issued by the Ministry of Sport in 2018 — [FIBO Arabia via Business News This Week](https://businessnewsthisweek.com/health/fibo-arabia-experts-outline-the-growth-of-women-in-fitness-in-saudi-arabia/). About 500 women's gym licences by 2022 (unverified, newsletter source) — [Build Free Capital Substack](https://buildfreecapital.substack.com/p/turning-ksa-into-la-this-gym-operator)
- Market size (vendor estimates, which vary): Saudi fitness services about USD 1.06bn in 2025 — [Nexdigm](https://www.nexdigm.com/market-research/insights/blog/ksa-fitness-service-industry-report/); about USD 1.5bn in 2026 — [GlobeNewswire / ResearchAndMarkets Jan 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/22/3223484/28124/en/Saudi-Arabia-Health-and-Fitness-Club-Market-Report-2026-2031-Subscription-Based-Membership-Fees-Drive-Nearly-80-of-Market-Revenue-but-Personalized-Training-Demand-Accelerates.html). Subscription fees are about 80% of market revenue (same source).
- Women are the fastest-growing segment, with a 13.05% CAGR forecast for 2026-2031. Men held 77.62% of the market in 2025 — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/saudi-arabia-health-and-fitness-club-market)
- Women enrolled in gyms are forecast to exceed 2.8 million by 2030 — [Nexdigm](https://www.nexdigm.com/market-research/insights/blog/ksa-fitness-service-industry-report/)
- Operators: Sports Club Company runs 17 Body Motions women's clubs and has a SAR 200m partnership to build female fitness centres in second-tier cities — [Build Free Capital](https://buildfreecapital.substack.com/p/turning-ksa-into-la-this-gym-operator) (unverified)
- Saudi creator signal: Dr Bakur Tamim (300k+ Instagram followers, 2024) began cooking because he "couldn't find enough healthy, delicious options to fuel his workouts" — [Arab News](https://arab.news/c6468). Ibrahim Al-Dosaimani has about 227k followers and 250+ healthy recipes filmed — [Arab News](https://arabnews.pk/node/2570379/saudi-arabia). A Saudi vegan cookbook author argues "protein is overhyped", showing that protein is a mainstream topic locally — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2589290)

### Inferences
- Roughly 4 in 10 Saudi women already meet the activity guideline, and the women's gym segment is growing about 13% per year. That gives a large and growing "gym girl" audience for high-protein recipes, even though men still dominate gym revenue.
- High-protein, macro-labelled recipes fit both the gym audience and the weight-loss audience, which makes this probably the widest single niche.

### Gaps
- No official 2024-2026 count of women's gym licences or gym memberships (Ministry of Sport / GASTAT).
- No gym participation data for UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Jordan, Iraq or Egypt.
- No regional data on protein product sales (e.g., protein yoghurt or protein snack growth in GCC).

## 3. Viral healthy-recipe niches and formats on Instagram/TikTok (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
"Protein everything" (cottage cheese, protein desserts, macro-labelled meal prep) is the biggest proven commercial niche, with billions of hashtag views and creators selling ebooks at USD 30-45. Fibre-maxxing/gut health is the fastest-rising 2025-2026 niche. PCOS and blood-sugar (Glucose Goddess-style) content are the largest women-specific health niches.

## 5. Which audiences save, share and buy recipe PDFs

### Takeaway
Hard data on recipe-PDF buyers is thin. Recipe content is used heavily (89% use digital recipes) and drives purchases (about 50-67% say they are likely to buy from recipe content). Creator benchmarks show high-protein/weight-loss audiences paying about USD 30-45 for ebooks. Women-specific condition audiences (PCOS, blood sugar) show the strongest engagement-to-need ratio.

### Cited Findings
- 89% of respondents use digital recipes, and the gender gap has halved since 2020 (Chicory 2025). Women are 53% of recipe users — [Progressive Grocer / Chicory](https://progressivegrocer.com/digital-recipes-attract-high-intent-grocery-shoppers); [Perishable News](https://perishablenews.com/retailfoodservice/chicorys-new-shopper-surveys-reveal-omnichannel-preferences-and-recipe-usage-patterns/)
- 67% are likely to make a grocery purchase directly from a digital recipe (Deloitte 2024, via SideChef) — [SideChef](https://www.sidechef.com/business/recipe-platform/recipe-platform-ecommerce-strategy). About 50% are likely or very likely (31% + 18%) to buy groceries from online food content (Chicory 2022) — [Chicory](https://chicory.co/blog-feed/omnichannel-grocery-shopper-2022-press-release)
- About 50% use recipe apps for meal planning, and about 70% of users are women (weak secondary source) — [ElectroIQ](https://electroiq.com/stats/recipe-app-statistics/)
- Ebook price benchmarks: USD 29.99-44.95 (Jalal), USD 30 (Gumroad) — [jalalsamfit.com](https://jalalsamfit.com/products/bundle-jalals-cookbook-mealprep-cookbook); [Gumroad](https://wolfeadam.gumroad.com/l/CompleteCookbook)
- The blood-sugar niche converted into 3M+ book sales (Glucose Goddess) — [Simon & Schuster](https://www.simonandschuster.ca/books/The-Glucose-Goddess-Method/Jessie-Inchauspe/9781668024522)

### Inferences

Suggested ranking of problem audiences for women 18-34 in KSA, Jordan, Iraq, Egypt and UAE. This is a judgement call combining prevalence, personal relevance to the age group, proven social reach and proven willingness to pay:

| Rank | Audience | Why (evidence above) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weight loss / obesity (women) | About 43-59% female obesity in the target countries; this is the core of every high-protein cookbook funnel |
| 2 | High-protein / gym women | 44.6% of Saudi women active; women's gym segment +13% CAGR; #highprotein 5.8bn views; proven USD 30-45 ebooks |
| 3 | PCOS / hormone balance | About 16-23% of GCC women, about 30% of infertile women; #PCOS 5.7bn views; age-matched to 18-34 |
| 4 | Blood sugar / insulin resistance / prediabetes | 20-26% adult diabetes in the Gulf and Egypt, 62-64% undiagnosed; Glucose Goddess sold 3M+ books |
| 5 | Diabetic-friendly (for parents) | Largest absolute numbers (Egypt 13.2M, KSA 5.3M); high share-to-family potential |
| 6 | Gut health / bloating / fibre | Rising fastest in 2026 (gut health +123% in searches); IBS at about 20% of Saudi women in Rome IV pooled data |
| 7 | Ramadan healthy (seasonal) | 68% search Ramadan content on TikTok; recipe searches spike about 50% in Ramadan (unverified) |
| 8 | Iron-deficiency anaemia | About 28-44% of women 15-49, but weak online identity |
| 9 | Gestational diabetes / pregnancy | 13-15% of pregnancies in MENA/GCC; narrow window, high need |
| 10 | Hypertension (low-salt) | High in Egypt (about 40%, 2011-12), older-skewing |
| 11 | Cholesterol | Older-skewing; weakest fit for women 18-34 |

- For the paid PDF, a combined weight-loss + high-protein + blood-sugar-friendly positioning covers ranks 1-4 at once. PCOS-friendly works well as a sub-section or a second product.

### Gaps
- No MENA-specific data on who buys digital cookbooks or meal plans, and no Instagram save/share rates by recipe niche.
- No data on whether the brand's half-English-fluent audience responds differently to English vs Arabic health labels.
